# M-SEASONTOUR — One minute, one year: a sample that shows every season, the frozen lake and the falling leaves in one run (milestone record)

**Status:** planned (2026-10-31), steps 22a to 22c. **Owner: routine 2 thread (the M-VISUAL line).** Follow-up of M-SEASONS (`Season`, `season_phase`, the foliage tint and density), M-FREEZE (`WaterIce`, `water_ice_amount`) and M-LEAFFALL (`LeafFall`, `leaf_fall_amount`), and of M-SNOWCOVER and M-WEATHER (the winter frost and the wind the leaves read). Today no single run shows the whole seasonal line: `samples/12_weather` has a `Season` with `year_days` 1 and a 600 s day, so its phase only climbs from 0.308 to 0.375 in the run and never reaches autumn (its `LeafFall` therefore sets `manual` 0.6 to keep the leaves drawn); `samples/13_water` has a 60 s year (`year_days` 30, a 2 s day, `start_phase` 0.6) but only the sea, so the ice shows at the start and the trees, the leaves and the snow are not there at all. No Linear issues (free issue limit); this record is the tracker.

**Goal.** A new data-only sample `samples/14_seasons`, played by the stock `mce_player` (assets plus one Luau script, no C++), that runs **one whole year in 60 s of real time** (3600 simulation ticks at 60 Hz): one scene with a ground, a small lake (`Water` plus `WaterIce`), a few trees (trunk boxes under `Foliage3D` canopies, so the `Season` tint and density act on them), a `SnowCover` and a `Weather` in clear weather (wind for the sway and the leaves), a `LeafFall` with `manual` 0 so the leaves fall **only because the season reaches autumn**, a fixed sun, and a slowly orbiting camera. A Luau script logs `season: phase %.3f frozen %.3f leaves %.3f at tick %d` every 150 ticks, so the headless test pins the four season centres (ticks 450, 1350, 2250, 3150) and the windowed run is the picture of the whole line: green spring, full summer, orange autumn with falling leaves, bare white winter with an ice-covered lake, thaw. It adds no engine code: it is the demo that closes the seasonal line and corrects the "demo scene whose `Season` really reaches autumn" follow-up of M-LEAFFALL.

**Not in scope:** any kit, render, script or shader change (if the sample shows that a component cannot do something, that is a recorded follow-up, not a fix here), a new component or Luau function, new assets (no new mesh, texture, sound or terrain asset: see Design), a `Terrain3D` / `TerrainFoliage3D` patch (they need a `.mcterrain` asset that no sample ships yet, see Risks), a weather forecast (the weather stays clear; rain and snow are the business of `12_weather`), a day and night cycle (the `WorldClock` has `drive_environment` 0 and the sun is fixed, as in `13_water`), sound, changing `12_weather` or `13_water` (their hashes, goldens and scenes stay byte-identical), and editing `season.h`, `leaf_fall.h`, `water_ice.h`, `kit3d.h`, `scene3d.h` or `weather.h`.

## Design

- **Real names read while planning.**
  - **Tick rate.** The sample logs say 60 ticks a second (`12_weather`: "60 ticks a second; transitions are 10 s = 600 ticks"; `13_water`: "a 2 s day, so a 60 s year" is 3600 ticks and its `ice:` lines run at ticks 0 to 2700 every 150). The sample therefore uses 3600 ticks, not 1800.
  - **Clock and season.** `WorldClock` (`mce/kit/world_clock.h`: `hour`, `day_length_seconds`, `time_scale`, `drive_environment`, the engine-written `day`, `phase`, `phase_den`) and `Season` (`mce/kit/season.h`: `year_days`, `start_phase`, `spring_tint`, `autumn_tint`, `winter_tint`, `winter_density`, `snow_cover_bias`, `cold_bias`, `strength`; the helpers `season_phase(const Season&, u32 day, f32 hour)`, `season_foliage_tint`, `season_snow_bias`, and the centres `kSeasonSpringCentre` 0.125, `kSeasonSummerCentre` 0.375, `kSeasonAutumnCentre` 0.625, `kSeasonWinterCentre` 0.875).
  - **Ice and leaves.** `WaterIce` (`mce/kit/water_ice.h`: `freeze_start` 0.5, `freeze_full` 0.9 on the winter weight, `manual`, `ice_color`, `calm`) and `LeafFall` (`mce/kit/leaf_fall.h`: the window `autumn_start` 0.5, `autumn_full` 0.6, `autumn_end_full` 0.65, `autumn_end` 0.75, `manual`, `rate`, `box_xz_m`, `height_m`).
  - **Trees.** `Foliage3D` (`mce/kit/kit3d.h`: `mesh`, `tint`, `half_extents`, `count`, `seed`, `scale_min/max`, `wind_*`, `cast_shadows`), which `read_season` in `kit3d_extract.cpp` tints and thins by the season, and `WindSway`.
  - **Script API.** The existing read-only getters `weather.season_phase()`, `weather.water_frozen()` and `weather.leaf_falling()`; `mce.find`, `mce.set`, `mce.tick`, `mce.log` as `samples/13_water/assets/scripts/water.luau` uses them; `water.height_at` is not needed.
  - **Wiring.** `samples/CMakeLists.txt` lists every sample with `add_subdirectory(<name>)` (`12_weather` and `13_water` sit next to `12_loot`); each sample's `CMakeLists.txt` cooks `assets/` with `mce_cook --pack`, adds `sample_<name>_headless_<n>` (a `cmake -P` script that runs `mce_player -headless 1 -ticks N`, greps the `script:` log lines and compares the `state_hash` of two plays) and, under `MCE_GPU_TESTS`, one windowed `sample_<name>_gpu` (`-capture_tick N -capture <file>.ppm`, label `gpu`, `mce_mark_slow`). `scripts/ci/capabilities.py` reads the `.mcscene` files of the samples; every component this sample uses is already named by `12_weather` or `13_water`, so the sample adds no capability row.
- **Scene (`samples/14_seasons/assets/scenes/seasons.mcscene`).** Entities, all with a `LocalTransform`: `Ground` (a `MeshRef` box scaled flat, grass tint), `Lakebed` (a darker box under the lake), `Sky` (`Environment3D` with a fixed sun low enough to light the canopies and the ice, as `13_water`'s `Sky`), `Camera` (`Camera3DComponent`, fov 55; the script moves it), `Lake` (`Water` with `half_extent` about 9 m so it is a pond, `level_y` 0, `amplitude` 0.15, plus `WaterIce` default; the lake entity also carries the clock and the season, as `13_water`'s `Water` entity does), `Weather` (`Weather` in clear weather with a light wind, `WindDirection`, `WindSway`, `SnowCover`, `LeafFall` with `manual` 0), `Tree 1` to `Tree 5` (a `MeshRef` trunk box in brown) and `Canopy 1` to `Canopy 5` (a `Foliage3D` of box "leaf clumps" over each trunk, 40 to 60 copies, `half_extents` about 2.5, green tint, `cast_shadows` 0 like `12_weather`), and `Script: seasons` (`ScriptSystem`, phase `Post`). About 20 entities; the exact count is pinned in 22b.
- **Clock and season numbers.** `WorldClock`: `hour` 0, `day_length_seconds` 2, `drive_environment` 0 (the clock never moves the fixed sun, as in `13_water`). `Season`: `year_days` 30, `start_phase` 0. A year is 30 days of 2 s = 60 s = 3600 ticks, and with `hour` 0 the phase is `tick / 3600`, so the four season centres fall on ticks 450 (spring 0.125), 1350 (summer 0.375), 2250 (autumn 0.625), 3150 (winter 0.875), all multiples of 150 (the log period). The script's tick N shows the state after N + 1 system ticks (the `12_weather` note), so the logged phase is 0.125 / 0.375 / 0.625 / 0.875 to three decimals; 22b measures the real lines and pins them. Other `Season` authoring (tints, `winter_density`, `snow_cover_bias`) keeps the defaults so the sample shows the stock look.
- **What each feature shows, expected from the helpers (22b measures, does not guess).** Leaves: the `LeafFall` window is 0 below phase 0.5, rises to 1 by 0.6, is 1 at 0.625 (tick 2250) and 0 from 0.75 (tick 2700). Ice: the winter weight is 0 below phase 0.5 and 1 at 0.875, the freeze is `smoothstep(0.5, 0.9, weight)`: it starts forming at about phase 0.69 (tick 2480), is full from about 0.80 to 0.90 (ticks 2890 to 3240), and is gone by about 0.94 (tick 3380), so winter is frozen and spring is open. The ice begins while the leaves are ending (ticks 2480 to 2700): this overlap is real and the sample shows it. Foliage: the green spring tint at tick 450, the neutral tint in summer, the orange `autumn_tint` at 2250, the grey `winter_tint` and the thinned canopy (`winter_density` 0.4) at 3150. Snow: `season_snow_bias` frosts the ground in winter through the `SnowCover` component with no precipitation (the `snow_cover_bias` floor).
- **Camera.** The script moves the `Camera` entity every tick (as `water.luau` moves the crate, with `mce.set` on `LocalTransform` `position` and `rotation`): a circle of radius about 14 m around the lake at a height of about 6 m, one revolution per 60 s (so one lap per year, 0.1 degree per tick), looking at a point just above the lake, the rotation a yaw about +y composed with a fixed downward pitch (a unit quaternion built in Luau from `math.sin` and `math.cos`). The orbit is a pure function of `mce.tick()`, so it is deterministic and the state hash is stable. The `LeafFall` box follows the camera, so the leaves always fall around the view; the radius is kept inside the canopy scatter so there is something to fall from.
- **Script (`assets/scripts/seasons.luau`).** `update()` moves the camera every tick and, when `t % 150 == 0`, logs `season: phase %.3f frozen %.3f leaves %.3f at tick %d` with `weather.season_phase()`, `weather.water_frozen()`, `weather.leaf_falling()`. It writes nothing else (no `weather.set_*`, no forecast), so every number is the authored component read through the clock. 24 lines in 3600 ticks (ticks 0 to 3450).
- **Assets and CREDITS.** No new assets and no license entry is needed: the samples build their world from the two boxes of `10_missions` (`floor.glb` and `platform.glb`, same bytes, plus their `.mceasset` sidecars with new guids in the sample's guid range), and `12_weather` / `13_water` ship exactly that with no `CREDITS.md` (the boxes are engine-generated, not third-party). 22b copies the two `.glb` files and writes the sidecars the way `13_water` did; it checks first that `samples/10_missions` has no credit line for them (`git grep CREDITS samples`) and, if a sibling sample ever added one, copies it. The trees are boxes under `Foliage3D`, not a downloaded model; a real tree prefab from `library_add` (Kenney, CC0, with a `CREDITS.md` line) is a follow-up.
- **Why the lake is not the terrain.** The ask for "a terrain patch" is met by the `Ground` box: `Terrain3D` and `TerrainFoliage3D` need a `.mcterrain` height-field asset that is cooked by a tool or generated by a script, and no sample under `samples/` ships one (`git grep mcterrain -- samples` is empty). A new terrain asset would be a new asset and an extra cook risk for no extra seasonal feature (both `Foliage3D` and `TerrainFoliage3D` read the same `read_season` tint). A terrain-based variant is a follow-up.
- **Determinism and hash.** The sample is a pure function of the scene and the tick: the clock is integer sub-steps, the season is exact from `day` and `hour`, the camera is a function of the tick, and nothing is random at run time (the foliage scatter is seeded). The headless test plays the pack twice and compares the `state_hash`, as every sibling does; the hash is stored in the log, not hard-coded, so a later kit change does not move a golden. No existing sample's bytes or hash change: the milestone only adds a directory and one `add_subdirectory` line.
- **Cost.** About 250 foliage copies, one `Water`, one particle emitter of at most 1024 leaves, one `SnowCover`: comparable to `12_weather` in the windowed run. The headless run of 3600 ticks is a few seconds. No new slow headless test (it is under 60 s; the full 3600-tick play is the gate and a 1800-tick smoke is not needed); the windowed captures are `slow` like their siblings.

## Gates
- `samples/14_seasons` is a directory plus one `add_subdirectory` line: every existing sample hash, golden and `ctest` expectation (including `sample_weather_*` and `sample_water_*`) stays byte-identical, and `docs/engine/capabilities.md` needs no new row.
- The `season:` lines are the whole contract: the four season centres at ticks 450, 1350, 2250, 3150, with the helpers' values pinned from a real run (measured, not computed by hand), and two plays give the same `state_hash`.
- `LeafFall.manual` is 0 and `WaterIce.manual` is 0 in the scene: every leaf and every bit of ice comes from the `Season` and the `WorldClock`. A scene that sets either manual floor fails review.
- The script writes no component except the `Camera` `LocalTransform` (no `weather.set_*`).
- No new asset format or license: the only binary files are the two box `.glb` copies of an existing sample (same bytes), with their sidecars.
- No edit to `season.h`, `leaf_fall.h`, `water_ice.h`, `kit3d.h`, `scene3d.h`, `weather.h` or any `src/` file; one module per PR (22a docs, 22b samples with docs rows).

## Risks
- **New sample, CMake registration.** A new directory is only built and tested when `samples/CMakeLists.txt` has its `add_subdirectory` line, and the `add_test` names must be unique: 22b adds the line and checks with `ctest -N -R seasons` that the three tests are listed (the dry listing does not need a build). The web and mobile branches of that file (`MCE_WEB`, `MCE_MOBILE`) return before the desktop list, so the new line goes after `13_water` in the desktop list only. `ctest -R water` and `ctest -R weather` must not pick the new tests up by accident (`sample_seasons_*` does not contain either word).
- **Generated files are the integration runner's.** `scripts/ci/sample_coverage.allow` and `docs/engine/capabilities.md` are regenerated by the integration run, not by the PR (CLAUDE.md): this sample adds no component, so neither is expected to change; if `capabilities.py --check` asks for a row (a component first named here), 22b says so in the PR and does not edit the generated file.
- **Windowed run is real time.** A windowed capture at tick 2250 plays 37.5 s of real time and at 3150 52.5 s; siblings saw stalls under loaded `--task` runs, so both captures are `slow` (the integration run executes them) and the headless pins are the PR gate. A frame is written for a look; there are no pixel goldens.
- **Wrong tick for a season.** The script's tick N shows the state after N + 1 system ticks and the phase has float rounding; 22b pins what the real run prints, prints three decimals and uses tick pairs far from an edge (the centres are far from every window edge, 0.1 wide), so a one-tick drift cannot flip a check.
- **Ice and leaves overlap.** The freeze starts at about phase 0.69 while the leaves last until 0.75; the sample shows leaves over a freezing lake for about 3.5 s. That is the helpers' real behaviour and it is documented, not tuned away (tuning would mean changing `freeze_start` or the window in the scene authoring, which the sample may do in 22c only if the look is bad).
- **The camera follow of the leaves.** The leaf box follows the camera, so on the orbit the leaves always fall in front of it, also over the lake and past the canopy. This is the M-LEAFFALL design (a box around the camera, not around trees) and it is recorded there; the guide paragraph says so.
- **Boxes are not trees.** Trunk boxes under box-clump canopies read as abstract trees; the colours carry the season, not the shapes. A real tree prefab (`library_add`, CC0, with a `CREDITS.md` line) or a `.mcterrain` patch are follow-ups.
- **Foliage and the weather entity.** `read_season` and the wind are read from the first `Season`, `WorldClock` and `Weather` in the world, wherever they are; the scene keeps one of each. If a canopy does not follow the season in the first windowed look, 22b records it instead of moving the scene around.
- **Entity count and the script name.** The `ScriptSystem` references the script by guid; the new guids must be in a range no other sample uses (`14d14000-...`, after `13d13000-...`), because the cook step reads guids from the sidecars and a duplicate guid between two samples is harmless only while each sample is cooked alone (they are).
