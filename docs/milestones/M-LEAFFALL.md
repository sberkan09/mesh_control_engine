# M-LEAFFALL — Drifting autumn leaves around the camera, from the season and the wind, with no shader (milestone record)

**Status:** planned (2026-10-31), steps 21a to 21d, PR column empty. **Owner: routine 2 thread (the M-VISUAL line).** Follow-up of M-SEASONS (the "leaf-fall particles in autumn" follow-up; `Season`, `season_phase`, `season_foliage_tint`), M-WEATHER and M-RAINFX (the particle feed of rain and snow, and `Weather.wind`), M-METEOR and M-FREEZE (the opt-in kit pattern: a pure-helper header, a drawn-only extract feed, no system, no shader). Today autumn is a foliage tint only: the trees turn orange but nothing ever falls. No Linear issues (free issue limit); this record is the tracker.

**Goal.** An opt-in kit component `LeafFall` turns the `Season` autumn weight (the phase quarter around 0.625, `kSeasonAutumnCentre`) at the `WorldClock` day and hour into a strength 0..1, with a manual floor for a scene that wants leaves all year. Pure helpers turn the strength into an emitter look: spawn rate, the drift velocity from the scene wind, leaf size, lifetime, gravity and drag (a slow, swaying fall) and a colour from the season foliage tint. `Kit3DExtract` adds one **render-side `render::ParticleEmitter`** that follows the camera, the same existing GPU particle path rain and snow use. No new shader, pass, render field or system state, and no component is written. A scene without `LeafFall` hashes, simulates and renders exactly as today.

**Not in scope:** a shader, a new `render::` field or struct change (leaf meshes, per-leaf spin, two-sided lit leaves or a leaf texture atlas all need one; the leaves are the existing camera-facing soft sprites, optionally with the authored sprite texture), leaves that spawn from specific trees or from `Foliage3D` copies (one box of leaves around the camera, see Design), leaves lying on the ground, collision with props, leaves swirling in a gust vortex, a leaf-fall system or any stored emitter state, changing the weather or the foliage by the leaves, sounds (rustle), and changing `Season`, `Weather`, `WindDirection`, `WindGust`, `Foliage3D`, `ParticleEmitter3D`, `Precipitation3D`, `Environment3D` or `Scene3D` bytes. `kit3d.h`, `scene3d.h`, `weather.h` and `season.h` are not edited.

## Design

- **Real names read while planning.** `render::ParticleEmitter` (`mce/render/scene3d.h`) is a plain per-frame description: `id`, `max_particles`, `seed`, `position`, `box`, `direction`, `spread`, `rate`, `burst`, `emitting`, `speed_min/max`, `lifetime_min/max`, `gravity`, `drag`, `collide`, `ground_height`, `bounce`, `size_start/end`, `color_start/end`, `additive`, `texture`. Forward3D keeps the particles of an emitter under its `id` across frames; one added with `scene.add_particle_emitter(em)` each extract. The `Kit3DExtract` loop over `ParticleEmitter3D` components (`kit3d_extract.cpp`, ~1965) fills one from the component and calls `add_particle_emitter`, counting `stats_.particle_emitters`; its ids are `e.index + 1`. Rain and snow reach it the other way round: the `kit_precipitation` system (`precipitation.cpp`) *writes* a `ParticleEmitter3D` component (`apply_emitter`, camera follow through `LocalTransform`), which is stateful and hashed. Leaf fall does **not** copy that: the extract builds the `render::ParticleEmitter` locally and adds it, so no component or entity is needed and no state is hashed. **Leaves need no shader and no render-struct change**: a slow-falling, drifting emitter of orange soft sprites is already expressible with the existing fields (`gravity` small, `drag` high, `direction` along the wind, `additive` false). `Season` (`mce/kit/season.h`) has `season_phase(const Season&, u32 day, f32 hour)`, `season_foliage_tint(const Season&, f32 phase) -> vec3`, the constant `kSeasonAutumnCentre` (0.625) and the internal `detail::season_smooth`, `detail::season_wrap`, `detail::season_winter_weight`; there is no autumn weight helper yet, 21a adds one inside `leaf_fall.h` (a smoothstep window around the autumn centre) and does not edit `season.h`. `Weather.wind` and `effective_wind(const Weather&, const WindGust*, consumer_bit)` (`wind_gust.h`), `wind_direction_vector(const WindDirection&)` (`wind_direction.h`) and `read_season` / the camera pose (`scene.view()`) are what the extract already uses for sway, snow and clouds.
- **Opt-in component, no system, no state.** New component `LeafFall` (`mce/kit/leaf_fall.h`, one per scene, the first in ECS order is read, registered by `install_weather` next to `Season` and `WaterIce`; 21a confirms the spot by reading `install_weather`). Pure data: the strength is a pure function of authoring and the clock, evaluated at extract and in the Luau getter. Authoring (all `Save`, with a `Range`): `enabled` 1; `autumn_start` 0.5 and `autumn_full` 0.6 (0..1, the phase where leaves start to fall and reach full strength; mirrored on the way out by `autumn_end_full` 0.65 and `autumn_end` 0.75, so the fall peaks in the middle of autumn, centre 0.625, and is gone by winter; inverted pairs are swapped); `manual` 0 (0..1, a floor for the strength, so a still scene or a script can drop leaves with no `Season`); `rate` 24 (0..500, leaves per second at full strength); `box_xz_m` 14 (1..100, half extent of the spawn box around the camera) and `height_m` 7 (1..50, spawn height above the camera); `size_m` 0.14 (0.02..1); `lifetime_s` 7 (1..30); `fall_speed` 0.9 (0..5, m/s, sets gravity and drag so the terminal speed is about this); `wind_follow` 1 (0..2, how strongly the scene wind pushes the leaves sideways); `leaf_color` (default 0.95 0.5 0.15, linear) and `tint_follow` 1 (0..1, how far the colour follows `season_foliage_tint` at the current phase, so leaves match the trees); `max_particles` 1024 (64..8192); `seed` 1; `strength` 1 (0..1, scales every effect; 0 = off exactly). No engine-written fields; naturally packed with an explicit zeroed pad and a `test_padding` entry.
- **Strength (kit, 21a).** `leaf_fall_amount(const LeafFall&, const Season*, f32 phase) -> f32` is `max(manual, window(phase))` times `strength`, with `window` a trapezoid of smoothsteps over `autumn_start..autumn_full` rising and `autumn_end_full..autumn_end` falling on the wrapped phase (the phase from `season_phase`, wrapped by the same arithmetic as `detail::season_wrap`; the window never crosses the year wrap because autumn is mid-year). With no `Season` the strength is the manual floor. NaN, inverted and zero ranges fall back to the defaults; the smoothstep is a polynomial (no `pow`, `exp` or trig, no `rand`), fixed evaluation order. `enabled` 0, `strength` 0 and a missing component (null pointer) return exactly 0.
- **Emitter look (kit, 21a).** `leaf_fall_look(const LeafFall&, f32 amount, f32 wind, const math::vec2& wind_dir, const math::vec3& foliage_tint) -> LeafFallLook {active, rate, speed_min, speed_max, direction, spread, gravity, drag, size, lifetime_min, lifetime_max, color_start, color_end, box, height}` with `a` the amount: `active = a > 0`; `rate = rate * a`; `gravity = (0, -g, 0)` with a small g and `drag` chosen so the terminal fall speed is `fall_speed` (the closed form `drag = g / fall_speed`, no iteration); the sideways drift is the wind: `direction = normalize((wind_dir.x * w, -1, wind_dir.y * w))` with `w = wind * wind_follow` and `speed_min/max` small multiples of `fall_speed` and of `w`; `spread` wide (about 0.6 rad) so leaves do not fall in lines; `size` from `size_m`; `lifetime` from `lifetime_s` (the box height over the fall speed is checked at the Range edges so a leaf does not die before it reaches the camera height); `color_start = leaf_color * lerp(1, foliage_tint, tint_follow)` with alpha 0 fading in, `color_end` the same colour with alpha 0 (alpha blended, `additive` false). At `a == 0` the look is `active = false` and zeros, which the bit-identical gates rely on. The extract does not call the helper when the amount is 0.
- **Extract (kit, 21b).** After the existing particle loop (or next to the snow cover block; 21b picks the spot by reading the file), the extract reads the first `LeafFall`, the first `Season`, the first `WorldClock` (day 0 hour 0 without a clock, as M-SEASONS and M-FREEZE do), the first `Weather` with `effective_wind(..., gust, bit)` and the first `WindDirection` once per frame (`.cpp` only), and the camera position from `scene.view()`. If the amount is above 0 it fills a local `render::ParticleEmitter` from the look, with `id` a fixed reserved value outside the `e.index + 1` range (for example `0xFFFF0001u`; 21b verifies no other id reaches it, the foliage batch ids use the top bits of a different id space), `position` at the camera plus `height_m` (a `follow_camera` always, as `Precipitation3D` defaults), `seed` and `max_particles` from authoring, `collide` false and no ground bounce (leaves just fade at the end of their life), and calls `scene.add_particle_emitter(em)`; it increments `stats_.particle_emitters`. A frame with amount 0 adds nothing (the emitter is simply not added, so Forward3D drops its particles, as for any emitter not added for a frame). `LeafFall`, `Season`, `WorldClock`, `Weather` and every `ParticleEmitter3D` are never written; the leaves are a render-side copy. `render::apply_quality` has no particle hook, so the Low tier handling is by the extract: it scales `rate` and `max_particles` by the tier through the existing quality struct if it already has a particle field, else a fixed 0.5 on Low (21b reads `quality_` first and records which).
- **Why no system and no component.** The strength is exact from the clock; a leaf-fall system would add state to hash, and a `ParticleEmitter3D` entity written per tick (as precipitation does) would put drawn leaves in the state hash and the save file. Particle positions live on the GPU ring and are not part of the simulation; two runs agree on the *emitter description* each frame, not on where individual leaves are, which is the same position every other particle effect is in.
- **Determinism and hash.** A scene without `LeafFall` is byte-identical (one empty query in the extract; the extract is not part of the state hash). A scene with it differs only in the saved component and in frames. The amount depends only on authoring, `day` and `hour`; the emitter description depends additionally on `Weather.wind`, the wind direction and the camera pose, all read-only. A test evaluates the helpers over a `WorldClock` run in one go and in two halves.
- **Quality tiers and cost.** A single emitter of at most 8192 particles, one extra draw of the existing particle pass. `r.particles 0` (if the console variable exists; 21b checks) and the Low tier keep their meaning.
- **Luau (21c).** `weather.set_leaf_fall{enabled=, autumn_start=, autumn_full=, autumn_end_full=, autumn_end=, manual=, rate=, box_xz_m=, height_m=, size_m=, lifetime_s=, fall_speed=, wind_follow=, leaf_color={r,g,b}, tint_follow=, max_particles=, seed=, strength=}`, getters `weather.leaf_fall_<key>()` (the colour returns `r, g, b`) and read-only `weather.leaf_falling()` (the current strength 0..1 from the same helper on the current `WorldClock` day and hour; the manual floor with no clock or no `Season`, nil-free), named errors, values clamped to the Range, nothing written on error, a missing `LeafFall` raises (the setter never adds one), typed in `src/engine/script/compiler/definitions.cpp` next to `weather.set_season` and `weather.set_water_ice`, same rules as those. `samples/12_weather` (it already has the `Season` and logs `season:`) gets a `LeafFall` on the Weather entity (its entity count is unchanged) and logs `leaves:` (the strength) every 150 ticks; its golden and log expectations move in 21c, nothing else. 21c reads the sample first: its `year_days` 1 and 600 s day, and whether its start phase passes through autumn within the golden's tick budget; if not, the sample uses `manual` for the checked frames.

## Gates
- A scene without `LeafFall` (or with `enabled` 0, `strength` 0, or an amount of 0) has an unchanged state hash and unchanged frames; every existing sample hash and golden stays byte-identical until 21a / 21c change `samples/12_weather`, and only that sample's expectations move.
- `Season`, `Weather`, `WindDirection`, `WindGust`, `WorldClock`, `ParticleEmitter3D`, `Precipitation3D` and `Environment3D` are never written by leaf code: the extract only adds a local `render::ParticleEmitter`; a test hashes the components and resources with and without `LeafFall`.
- The amount and every helper are pure functions of authoring, `day` and `hour` (the look also of the read-only wind and camera inputs it is handed); no stored float, no wall clock, no `rand`.
- No shader, pass, `PassU` change or new render field is added; the only render-side value is an existing `render::ParticleEmitter`.
- `kit3d.h`, `scene3d.h`, `weather.h` and `season.h` are not edited (checked with `scripts/ci/include_fanout.py`); `leaf_fall.h` is included only by the extract, the install, the script binding and the tests, so no seam PR is expected.
- Luau: setters clamp, bad types raise named errors and write nothing.

## Risks
- **Sprites, not leaves.** The leaves are camera-facing soft dots or the authored sprite texture: no spin, no leaf shape, no lit two-sided shading. At 0.14 m they read as orange flecks; a texture and a size range are tuned against the `12_weather` view in 21c. Real leaf meshes are a render milestone.
- **A box around the camera, not around trees.** Leaves fall in a volume that follows the camera, so they also fall in a clearing, indoors and over water. There is no foliage lookup; the guide says to author `manual` 0 and rely on the season, or place the scene accordingly. Spawning from tree canopies is a recorded follow-up (it would need foliage positions in the extract and a per-batch emitter).
- **No collision.** Leaves pass through floors and roofs; they die by lifetime near the ground (`lifetime_s` is checked against the box height). `collide` stays false.
- **GPU particles are not hashed.** Positions of individual leaves are not deterministic across machines and are not in the state hash; only the emitter description is, by construction, a pure function. A headless run draws none.
- **Id range.** The reserved emitter id must not collide with `e.index + 1`; 21b checks the entity index bound and the other emitter users, and a test pins it.
- **Needs a driver.** With no `Season` and `manual` 0 nothing falls; with no `WorldClock` the season is fixed at `start_phase`. With the default 1200 s day a 12-day year is 4 real hours, so the sample shortens `year_days` or the day length.
- **Wind coupling.** The drift reads `effective_wind` and `WindDirection`; a storm in autumn blows the leaves sideways hard. The look clamps the drift so the leaves stay inside the box; `wind_follow` 0 turns the coupling off.
- **Snow and leaves together.** A winter `manual` floor shows leaves over snow; the window ends before winter, and the guide states that `manual` overrides the season.
