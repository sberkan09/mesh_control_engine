# M-OUTLINE — Edge outlines: ink lines on silhouettes and creases from the opaque prepass, for a stylized or toon look (milestone record)

**Status:** planned (2026-11-04), steps 39a to 39f (39a done, the rest not started). **Owner: routine 2 thread (the M-VISUAL line).** Follow-up of M-LENSFLARE (the same opt-in post-pass pattern over the half-resolution opaque prepass) and of the 3D visual assessment (`assessments/3d-gorsellik-degerlendirmesi.md`: the engine's strength today is "stylize / low-poly / indie 3D", and nothing in the 3D path gives that look its signature line work). Today the renderer draws vignette, grain, chromatic aberration and lens dirt (M-VISUAL3 3), bloom, depth of field, motion blur, TAA, auto exposure, SSR, SSAO, contact shadows, volumetric fog and light shafts, clouds, water, the sky phenomena and the sun lens flare, and has no edge or outline pass: the only outlines in the engine are 2D text outlines (`TextLabel::outline_width`, `sprite_batch.h` `outline_px`) and the editor's selection overlay quads. No Linear issues (free issue limit, Linear unreachable); this record is the tracker. The pass step (39c) is Opus-written and merged after an independent Opus review plus a green `--task`, as M-LENSFLARE 38c, M-CLOUDS 9b and M-AURORA 16c were.

**Why this one (2026-11-04 survey).** Checked against `src/`, `projects/`, `tests/`, `samples/` and `docs/` with grep before choosing: camera film effects (vignette, grain, chromatic aberration) already exist in the tonemap pass (`PostFx::Lens`); light shafts exist through `Volumetrics`; cascade blending exists (`ShadowSettings::blend`); reflection probes and SSR exist. Absent: edge outlines (no `outline`, `sobel`, `edge_detect`, `toon` or `cel` in the 3D render path), light cookies, water caustics, heat haze, DoF bokeh shapes, planar mirrors. Of these, the edge outline has the largest visible effect per unit of risk: it changes the whole frame's style with one component, it reuses the prepass the flare already reads (view normal and view depth), it is one fullscreen pass with no new resource type, and it serves the engine's actual audience (stylized indie 3D). Light cookies need a texture array bank and a lighting-loop change in every lit shader variant; caustics and heat haze are narrower (water and hot scenes only); bokeh refines an existing effect; a planar mirror needs an extra scene pass. Those stay candidates for later milestones.

**Naming:** no type, file or milestone called `EdgeOutline`, `EdgeOutline3D`, `edge_outline`, `Outline3D`, `InkOutline` or `M-OUTLINE` exists (checked 2026-11-04). The render setting is `render::EdgeOutline` (in `PostFx::outline`), the pure helpers live in `mce/render/edge_outline.h`, the kit component is `EdgeOutline3D` (`mce/kit/edge_outline.h`), the cvar is `r.edge_outline`.

**Goal.** An opt-in post pass draws lines where the opaque scene has a silhouette (a jump in view depth) or a crease (a jump in the view normal), in an authored colour and opacity, a thickness in output pixels, fading out with distance and with the exp² fog. It runs on the HDR frame right after the MSAA resolve and **before TAA**, so TAA anti-aliases the lines and keeps them stable under its jitter, and depth of field, motion blur, bloom, the tonemapper and the grade act on them like on any scene pixel. **Render-side only: nothing is written to a component and nothing is hashed.** With `PostFx::outline.enabled` false (the default), no `EdgeOutline3D`, `r.edge_outline 0` or `Forward3DDesc::edge_outline` false, the pass is not recorded and every frame and golden is bit-identical.

**Not in scope:** per-object outlines or a selection highlight (a per-draw mask in the prepass, an ID or stencil channel; the obvious next milestone, see Follow-ups), per-object exclusion (foliage, particles), cel or toon shading of the lighting itself (banded diffuse, rim light), outlines of transparent surfaces, particles or decals (the edges come from the opaque prepass only), hand-drawn or textured strokes, line width that varies with distance or curvature, normal-mapped creases (the prepass writes the vertex normal, so only geometric creases count), a Luau API, the MCP `render_frame` `environment` override and the editor viewport (the editor draws its own preview path).

## Design

- **Render setting (seam, 39a).** A top-level POD `render::EdgeOutline` in `scene3d.h`, just above `PostFx` (beside `LensFlare`), and a member `EdgeOutline outline;` in `PostFx` after `flare`. Fields, defaults and the ranges `apply_quality` clamps to (NaN falls back to the default):

  | Field | Default | Range | Meaning |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | `bool enabled` | false | | off draws the frame without the pass |
  | `math::vec3 color` | {0.02, 0.02, 0.025} | 0..16 per channel | line colour, linear HDR (values above 1 glow under bloom) |
  | `f32 opacity` | 1.0 | 0..1 | how far a full edge replaces the pixel with `color` |
  | `f32 thickness` | 1.0 | 0.5..4 | tap distance in output pixels (the line is about twice this wide) |
  | `f32 depth_threshold` | 0.05 | 0.001..1 | relative view-depth jump (`abs(d_n - d_c) / d_c`) that starts a silhouette edge |
  | `f32 normal_threshold` | 0.4 | 0.01..2 | `1 - dot(n_c, n_n)` that starts a crease edge |
  | `f32 depth_weight` | 1.0 | 0..1 | strength of silhouette edges (0 = none) |
  | `f32 normal_weight` | 1.0 | 0..1 | strength of crease edges (0 = none) |
  | `f32 fade_start` | 30.0 | 0..10000 | view depth (m) where lines start to fade |
  | `f32 fade_end` | 80.0 | `fade_start`..10000 | view depth (m) where lines are gone |
  | `bool sky_silhouette` | true | | geometry against the sky gets a silhouette line (the sky counts as infinitely far) |
  | `bool fog_follow` | true | | lines fade in the exp² `Fog` (`Scene3D::fog().density`); ignored while `Volumetrics` is on |

  `scene3d.h` is at 177 of 986 TUs and `forward3d.h` at 159 (`scripts/ci/include_fanout.py`, 2026-11-04, both over the 150 warning), so 39a is the seam PR for both, data only, merged first, with the numbers re-measured in its description. In `forward3d.h` it adds `bool edge_outline = true;` to `Forward3DDesc` (the outline pipelines are created, and with them the prepass pipelines, see 39c), `bool edge_outline = false;` and `u32 edge_outline_taps = 0;` to `Forward3DStats`, and the private members 39c needs (`outline_pipe_`, `outline_dst_` (its own render-size target, see Pass design) and the declaration `void record_outline(rhi::CommandBuffer&, const Scene3D&);`); the handles go into the existing destroy lists in `forward3d.cpp` so they are referenced. No later step touches either header.
- **Pure CPU helpers (render, 39b).** New narrow header `mce/render/edge_outline.h` (includes `mce/math` only, forward-declares `struct EdgeOutline;`), bodies in `src/edge_outline.cpp`; f32, fixed evaluation order, no `rand`, no state. The three per-tap terms are the reference the shader mirrors literally (one expression each, pinned by the CPU tests, checked line by line in the 39c review):
  - `inline constexpr u32 kEdgeOutlineMaxTaps = 8;` and the fixed tap table `kEdgeOutlineTaps[8]` (the cross (+x, -x, +y, -y) first, then the four diagonals at `1/sqrt(2)` length so every tap is `thickness` pixels away).
  - `u32 edge_outline_tap_count(bool wide)`: 4 (cross) or 8 (cross and diagonals).
  - `f32 edge_outline_depth_term(f32 d_center, f32 d_tap, f32 threshold, f32 n_dot_v, bool sky_silhouette)`: the silhouette strength in [0, 1]. Depth 0 means sky (the prepass clear): a sky center gives 0 (lines stay on geometry, never in the sky); a sky tap gives 1 when `sky_silhouette`, else 0. Otherwise only the nearer side draws (`d_tap > d_center`, so the line sits inside the front object's outline and never doubles), with `smoothstep(threshold, 2 * threshold, (d_tap - d_center) / d_center * max(n_dot_v, 0.2))`: the `n_dot_v` factor raises the effective threshold on surfaces seen at a grazing angle, where depth changes fast without an edge (the floor in the distance).
  - `f32 edge_outline_normal_term(math::vec3 n_center, math::vec3 n_tap, f32 threshold)` = `smoothstep(threshold, 1.5 * threshold, 1 - dot(n_center, n_tap))`; 0 when either side is sky.
  - `f32 edge_outline_fade(f32 view_depth, f32 fade_start, f32 fade_end, f32 fog_density)` = `(1 - smoothstep(fade_start, fade_end, d)) * exp(-(fog_density * d)^2)`; the fog factor matches the exp² `Fog` (`fog_density` is 0 when `Volumetrics` is on, see Risks).
  - `struct EdgeOutlineFrame { math::vec4 color_opacity; math::vec2 tap_uv; f32 depth_threshold; f32 normal_threshold; f32 depth_weight; f32 normal_weight; f32 fade_start; f32 fade_end; f32 fog_density; u32 taps; bool sky_silhouette; f32 strength; };`
  - `EdgeOutlineFrame edge_outline_frame(const EdgeOutline&, u32 width, u32 height, f32 fog_density, bool wide)`: everything the pass needs, with `tap_uv = thickness / {width, height}` in output uv; `strength` = `opacity` when the pass should draw and exactly 0 for `enabled` false, `opacity` 0, both weights 0, `fade_end <= 0`, a zero-size view or NaN input (the pass skips on 0). The GPU receives only these numbers and the three term functions are mirrored, not tabled, so there is no table to keep in sync.
- **Quality tiers (render, 39b).** The outline is art direction, not a luxury, so no tier turns it off: `QualitySettings::edge_outline` (true on every tier) and `QualitySettings::edge_outline_wide` (Low false: the 4-tap cross; Medium and High true: 8 taps). Cvar `r.edge_outline` (bool, Saved, "Edge outlines (PostFx::outline; off = the frame without them)") next to `r.lens_flare` in `quality.cpp`. The existing `apply_quality(q, ShadowSettings&, PostFx&)` clamps the `outline` ranges (and orders `fade_end >= fade_start`) and turns `enabled` off for `!q.edge_outline` or `r.edge_outline 0`; `quality.h` (119 TUs, under the warning) only gains the two fields.
- **Kit component (kit, 39d).** Opt-in `EdgeOutline3D` (`mce/kit/edge_outline.h`, one per scene, the first in ECS order is read, registered by `install_kit3d` next to `LensFlare3D`; a separate component so the wide `kit3d.h`, 245 TUs, is not touched). Fields (all `Save`, with the `Range` above): `enabled` 1, the colour, `opacity`, `thickness`, `depth_threshold`, `normal_threshold`, `depth_weight`, `normal_weight`, `fade_start`, `fade_end`, `sky_silhouette` 1, plus `fog_follow` 1 and an explicit zeroed `pad_`. There is no kit-side formula (the fog density and the volumetrics check are the renderer's, from `Scene3D::fog()` and `Scene3D::volumetrics()`), so the component is a field copy; the colour is stored as three `f32` (`color_r`, `color_g`, `color_b`, linear) to keep the column POD and the inspector's `Range` per channel. `Kit3DExtract::apply_environment` (`.cpp` only) copies the fields into `post.outline` before `scene.set_post(post)` and passes the filled setting through `render::apply_quality(quality_, ShadowSettings&, PostFx&)` on a throwaway `PostFx` with only `outline` copied back (the 38h pattern), so the tier, `r.edge_outline 0` and the `Range` clamps apply at the extract, not only in the renderer; with no `EdgeOutline3D` or `enabled` 0 the default (disabled) setting is written, so a stale one never survives.
- **Determinism.** The outline is a pure function of the camera, this frame's prepass and the settings; it never feeds the simulation, a component or the state hash. A scene without `EdgeOutline3D` is byte-identical (one empty query in the extract, which is not hashed); a scene with it differs only in the saved component and in frames.

## Pass design (39c)

- **Where it sits.** `record_outline(cmd, scene)` runs in `Forward3D::record_view` after the MSAA resolve and the velocity prepass and before `record_taa`. So TAA resolves the lines (the prepass is drawn with the same jittered projection as the main pass, so the edges jitter with the frame and TAA averages them into anti-aliased lines), and depth of field, motion blur, the exposure histogram, the flare, bloom, the tonemapper and the grade act on them. `record_taa` binds `post_source_` instead of `hdr_` as its current frame (the same handle when the outline did not draw, so a frame without it is bit-identical); the reflections of the next frame keep reading `hdr_` (the frame without lines; 39c checks which handle SSR copies and records it). Its time falls in the existing `taa` timing bucket (`kTsResolve`..`kTsTaa`); no new timestamp, no header change.
- **CPU side.** `edge_outline_frame(scene.post().outline, size.width, size.height, fog, quality_settings(quality_tier()).edge_outline_wide)` with `fog = setting.fog_follow && !scene.volumetrics().enabled ? scene.fog().density : 0`; `strength == 0` returns before recording anything (bit-identical frame, `stats_.edge_outline` false).
- **Source.** The main depth buffer is a non-sampled (possibly MSAA) `DepthTarget`, so the pass reads the half-resolution opaque prepass `ao_gbuf_` (`RGBA16F`: `rgb` the view-space vertex normal, `a` the linear view depth, 0 for sky), calling `record_prepass` (a no-op when SSAO, SSR, contact shadows, the water or the flare drew it already this frame). Init creates the prepass pipelines when `ssao || ssr || contact || outline` (today only the first three), so the outline also works on a renderer without SSAO; `reserve_instancing` counts it as a prepass user, as it does the flare. This also closes the M-LENSFLARE follow-up "the flare without SSAO, SSR and contact shadows at init" for any renderer that keeps `edge_outline` (39c records whether the water's depth read is unaffected).
- **Target.** Its own render-size `RGBA16F` target `outline_dst_` (created on first use and on a resize), then `post_source_ = outline_dst_`. Not `post_fx_target`: its ping-pong pair is sized to `post_size_`, and with TAA upscaling (render scale below 1) the post size after TAA differs from the render size before it, so sharing it would recreate the pair twice a frame. Never in place into `hdr_`.
- **Composite (`kOutlineFS`).** One fullscreen pass: for the pixel's prepass texel (point sampled at the pixel's uv) and each of `taps` neighbours at `uv + kEdgeOutlineTaps[k] * tap_uv` (a constant-bound loop of 8 with an early `continue` past `taps`, `textureLod(..., 0.0)`), `e = max(e, max(depth_weight * depth_term, normal_weight * normal_term))`; then `a = strength * e * fade(d_center)` and `out = mix(src, color, a)` with the source alpha kept. A sky center draws nothing. Taps outside [0, 1] clamp to the edge texel (the sampler's clamp), which reads as no edge.
  A uniform block `OutlineU` (bank `kPass`; 5 `vec4`: colour and opacity, `tap_uv` and the source texel size, the two thresholds and two weights, the fade start, end, fog density and tap count, and `sky_silhouette` and strength; 80 bytes) is declared in `forward3d.cpp` next to `FlareU` and in `shaders3d.h` with `static_assert`s on the size and offsets. No change to `PassU`, `FrameU` or any existing block.
- **Per backend.** The fragment shader is GLSL 450 in `shaders3d.h`, like every pass: GL compiles it as it is; SDL GPU goes GLSL → SPIR-V (glslang) for Vulkan and SPIR-V → MSL (`rhi_sdlgpu/src/shader_msl.cpp`) on Apple; the Metal backend translates it with `rhi_metal/src/shader_cross.cpp` (`translate_glsl`); WebGPU goes through `rhi_webgpu/src/shader_wgsl.cpp`. To stay portable it uses only what the flare and the post passes use: combined samplers at the existing banks (`kAlbedo` for the source, `kNormal` for `ao_gbuf_`), one uniform block at `kPass`, no SSBO, no compute, no `textureGather`, explicit `textureLod`, no derivatives, constant loop bounds. The uv convention is checked with an asymmetric scene (a box in the upper left) on GL and SDL GPU so a flipped v shows as a test failure. A `test_shader_spirv` case compiles it and checks the binding table (Linux, no GPU); a `test_shader_cross` case translates it (macOS only); WebGPU translation is checked if its target builds in the container, else recorded as a follow-up, as for the flare.

## Cost and budget

- **GPU work when on.** One fullscreen pass at render size: 1 source fetch plus 5 (Low) or 9 (Medium, High) point fetches of the half-resolution `RGBA16F` prepass per pixel, a handful of ALU per tap; about the cost of the FXAA pass. Target on a mid GPU at 1080p High: at most 0.25 ms for the pass (measured on the GPU list, not here). Plus the half-resolution opaque prepass when nothing else on the frame drew it (SSAO, SSR, contact shadows, water and the flare share one), which is the larger cost on Low; recorded in the risks.
- **Memory.** One render-size `RGBA16F` target (about 16 MB at 1080p), created only once the outline draws.
- **CPU.** One `edge_outline_frame` call per view and frame, a few dozen flops; nothing per object.
- **Off.** Nothing: no target, no pipeline bind, no prepass on its account (the pipelines are created at init when `Forward3DDesc::edge_outline` is true, a one-time shader compile).

## Risks

- **Half-resolution source.** Edges come from a half-resolution prepass, so a line is at least two output pixels wide and steps at half resolution. With TAA the jitter averages it into a smooth line; without TAA, FXAA runs after the tonemap and softens it. A thin object under two pixels wide may get a broken line. Recorded; a full-resolution edge source (the velocity prepass's depth when it runs, plus a normal) is a follow-up.
- **Prepass cost on Low.** Low usually runs without SSAO, SSR and contact shadows, so on Low the outline alone pays for the half-resolution opaque prepass. Accepted because the outline is part of a game's look, not a quality option; the 4-tap kernel keeps the pass itself cheap, and `r.edge_outline 0` gives the player the frame without it. The real cost goes to the GPU list.
- **Foliage and alpha-tested meshes.** The prepass draws alpha-tested leaves (with their discard), so dense foliage can fill with lines. The default thresholds are chosen on the tour's trees in 39e; per-object exclusion is a follow-up.
- **Transparent surfaces and fog.** Lines come from the opaque prepass and are drawn over the composed frame, so the edges of an object behind glass or water show through it at full strength, and with `Volumetrics` on the lines do not fade in the volumetric fog (only `fade_start` / `fade_end` apply; the exp² fog factor is used only when volumetrics are off). Recorded as limits; the distance fade covers the common case.
- **Exposure.** Dark lines lower the frame's average luminance a little, so auto exposure brightens slightly with the outline on. Accepted; documented in 39f.
- **Look.** The default thresholds, the colour and the thickness are look choices that only a real display can judge; llvmpipe pixels prove placement, the off-checks and stability, not taste.

## GPU verification

What the headless tests (Mesa llvmpipe for GL, lavapipe for SDL GPU, in the container) can prove:
- **39b (no GPU):** the tap table (eight unit-length directions at `thickness`, cross first); the tap count 4 or 8; the depth term (0 for a sky center, 1 for a sky tap with `sky_silhouette`, 0 without it, 0 when the tap is nearer, the smoothstep edges at `threshold` and `2 * threshold`, the grazing-angle factor); the normal term (0 for equal normals, 1 for perpendicular ones at the default threshold); the fade (1 before `fade_start`, 0 after `fade_end`, the exp² fog factor); `strength` exactly 0 for each off case; the clamps (including `fade_end < fade_start` reordered and NaN to default) and the tier values; `r.edge_outline 0` disables.
