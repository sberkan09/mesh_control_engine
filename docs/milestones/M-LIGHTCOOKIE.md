# M-LIGHTCOOKIE — Light cookies: a texture projected by a spot light (and the sun) that shapes its light, for window grids, foliage dapples and flashlight masks (milestone record)

**Status:** done (2026-11-05), steps 40a to 40i. Real-GPU checks (look, cost, Metal, WebGPU) stay in the GPU backlog row A13. **Owner: routine 2 thread (the M-VISUAL line).** Follow-up of M-VISUAL 4 (local-light shadows: the spot atlas), M-OUTLINE (the same opt-in, render-side-only pattern, and its survey that named light cookies as the next candidate: "a texture array bank and a lighting-loop change in every lit shader variant") and the 3D visual assessment (`assessments/3d-gorsellik-degerlendirmesi.md`: a spot light is a clean cone with a soft edge and nothing else, so a lamp behind a window, a flashlight or sun through leaves cannot be made without a mesh that casts a shadow). Today `render::PointLight` is a point light, or a spot when `spot_outer` > 0, with a cone falloff (`spot_inner`, `spot_outer`) and an optional shadow tile (`cast_shadows`, `LocalShadowSettings`); the sun is a `DirectionalLight` with cascades; the kit has `SpotLight3D` and no way to give its light a pattern. No Linear issues: free limit; this record is the tracker. The shader steps (40c to 40f) are Opus-written and merged after an independent Opus review plus a green `--task`, as M-OUTLINE 39c, M-LENSFLARE 38c, M-CLOUDS 9b and M-AURORA 16c were.

**Naming:** no type, file, shader symbol or milestone called `cookie`, `Cookie`, `LightCookie`, `SunCookie`, `gobo` or `M-LIGHTCOOKIE` exists (`src/`, `docs/`, `tests/`, `samples/` checked with grep 2026-11-04; the only hit is the one word "light cookies" in `docs/milestones/M-OUTLINE.md`'s survey paragraph, which names this milestone's topic). The render fields are `PointLight::cookie*` (in `clusters.h`) and `DirectionalLight::cookie` of the new POD `render::SunCookie` (in `scene3d.h`), the pure helpers live in `mce/render/light_cookie.h`, the kit components are `LightCookie3D` (a spot light's cookie, `mce/kit/light_cookie.h`) and `SunCookie3D` (the sun's, same header), the cvar is `r.light_cookies`.

**Goal.** An opt-in texture modulates the light of a spot light or the sun:

- **Spot.** The texture is projected from the light through the spot's cone (a square perspective frustum of full angle `2 * spot_outer`, so the cone's circle is inscribed and every lit pixel has a texel), optionally rolled about the axis. The sample multiplies the light's colour (rgb, so a stained-glass cookie tints) and is mixed in by `cookie_strength`. It acts in the forward shader's local-light loop of the mesh and terrain passes, **before the local shadow lookup** (a black texel skips the nine shadow taps), and in the volumetric fog's light injection, so a window grid also draws light shafts.
- **Sun.** The texture tiles over the world plane perpendicular to the sun direction (a world-space size in metres per tile, an offset, a scroll velocity), multiplies the sun's term in the lit passes, and so gives leaf dapples or moving cloud shade. It does not touch the cascades, the contact shadows or the sky: it is the pattern of the sun's light, not an occluder.

**Render-side only: nothing is written to a component and nothing is hashed.** With no light carrying a cookie, `r.light_cookies 0`, `Forward3DDesc::light_cookies` false or the Scene without a cookie key, no atlas is created, no bake pass is recorded, the shaders take the not-taken branch, and every frame and golden is bit-identical.

**Not in scope:** point-light cookies (a cube map: six faces, a different projection; follow-up), a cookie on a shadow-less area or tube light (no such light exists), animated cookies as video or sprite sheets (the kit rolls and scrolls a still texture; flipbooks are a follow-up), cookies that cast through transparent surfaces (the cookie rides on the light, the volumetric injection is the only participating-media term), a cookie on the sky's sun disk (the disk is drawn by the sky pass), per-light mip or filter choice, procedural cookies (noise-generated shapes), IES profiles (a different data type, a 1D photometric curve; a natural follow-up on the same light-record slot), a Luau API, the MCP `render_frame` `environment` override and the editor viewport (the editor draws its own preview path). Reach from scripts and agents is the component reflection every kit component already has (see Design, Reach).

## Design

- **Per-light data (seam, 40a).** `render::PointLight` (in `clusters.h`) gains four members after `cast_shadows`; they mean something only for a spot (`spot_outer` > 0) and a point light ignores them:

  | Field | Default | Range | Meaning |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | `u32 cookie` | `~0u` | | `TextureStore` key of the cookie (rgb used, a ignored; any 2D texture); `~0u` = none |
  | `f32 cookie_strength` | 1.0 | 0..1 | 0 = the plain light, 1 = fully shaped; the sample is `mix(1, tex.rgb, strength)` |
  | `f32 cookie_roll` | 0.0 | radians, wrapped | rotation of the projection about the cone axis |
  | `f32 cookie_edge` | 0.0 | 0..1 | fades the cookie out toward the frustum border so a cookie wider than the cone has no hard cut (0 = off) |

  and `render::SunCookie` in `scene3d.h`, a top-level POD above `DirectionalLight`, with `SunCookie cookie;` as a new member at the end of `DirectionalLight`:

  | Field | Default | Range | Meaning |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | `u32 texture` | `~0u` | | `TextureStore` key; `~0u` = none |
  | `f32 strength` | 1.0 | 0..1 | as above |
  | `f32 size` | 20.0 | 0.5..1000 | world metres one repeat of the texture covers |
  | `math::vec2 offset` | {0, 0} | any | world metres the pattern is shifted along the plane's two axes |
  | `f32 roll` | 0.0 | radians | rotation of the pattern in the plane |

  The sun plane's axes are fixed from the sun direction (`right = normalize(cross(up_hint, d))`, `up_hint = +Y` unless the sun is within about 1 degree of vertical, then +Z), so the pattern does not swim when the sun moves a hair. *Amended in the 40e review:* that basis snaps at the up-hint switch near the zenith and makes the pattern on the ground swim with the sun's azimuth; the pattern now lives on the ground plane, each point carried to y = 0 along the sun's direction (`(x - y d.x / d.y, z - y d.z / d.y)`, `|d.y|` floored at `kSunCookieMinDy` = 0.05, about 2.9 degrees, for a grazing sun), so the dapples on flat ground stay where they are whatever the sun does. Scrolling is the kit's: `SunCookie3D::scroll` advances `offset` in the extract with the same clock the cloud layer uses; the renderer sees only the resulting `offset`.

  `clusters.h` is at 183 of 992 TUs, `scene3d.h` at 183 and `forward3d.h` at 163 (`scripts/ci/include_fanout.py`, 2026-11-04, all over the 150 warning), so 40a is the seam PR for all three, data only, merged first, with the numbers re-measured in its description. In `forward3d.h` it adds `bool light_cookies = true;` to `Forward3DDesc` (the bake pipeline and the cookie atlas may be created, lazily), `u32 cookie_lights = 0;`, `u32 cookie_layers = 0;` and `bool sun_cookie = false;` to `Forward3DStats`, and the private members 40c needs (`cookie_pipe_`, `cookie_atlas_`, `cookie_keys_`, `cookie_records_` and the declarations `void record_cookie_bake(rhi::CommandBuffer&, const Scene3D&);` and `u32 upload_cookie_records(rhi::CommandBuffer&, const Scene3D&);`); the handles go into the existing destroy lists in `forward3d.cpp` so they are referenced. `GpuPointLight` keeps its 64 bytes: the cookie rides in the unused `shadow.zw` (see Binding), so the `static_assert(sizeof(GpuPointLight) == 64)` and the cluster build (CPU and `cluster_build.comp`) are untouched. No later step touches any of the three headers or `kit3d.h`.
- **Pure CPU helpers (render, 40b).** New narrow header `mce/render/light_cookie.h` (includes `mce/math` only, forward-declares `struct PointLight; struct SunCookie; struct DirectionalLight;`), bodies in `src/light_cookie.cpp`; f32, fixed evaluation order, no `rand`, no state. The shader mirrors the two projection helpers literally (one expression each, pinned by the CPU tests, checked line by line in the 40d and 40e reviews):
  - `math::mat4 spot_cookie_matrix(math::vec3 position, math::vec3 direction, f32 spot_outer, f32 roll, f32 radius)`: world to cookie clip (GL convention, like `LocalShadowTile::view_proj`): a look-at along `direction` with an up vector that is +Y unless the cone points within about 1 degree of vertical (then +Z), rolled by `roll`, and a perspective with a vertical field of view of `2 * spot_outer`, aspect 1, near `0.05`, far `radius`. The texture lookup is `uv = clip.xy / clip.w * 0.5 + 0.5` and a pixel is inside when `clip.w > 0` and both coordinates are in [0, 1].
  - `f32 spot_cookie_edge(math::vec2 uv, f32 edge)` = 1 when `edge` is 0, else `smoothstep(0, edge * 0.5, min(min(uv.x, 1 - uv.x), min(uv.y, 1 - uv.y)))`.
  - `math::vec2 sun_cookie_uv(math::vec3 world, math::vec3 sun_dir, const SunCookie&)`: `(dot(world, right), dot(world, up)) / size + offset / size`, rolled (*amended in the 40e review:* the ground point `(x - y d.x / dy, z - y d.z / dy)` in place of the plane axes, see above), with the repeat done by the sampler (`REPEAT` on the cookie array; see Binding for the spot clamp).
  - `f32 cookie_lod(f32 pixel_footprint_m, f32 distance_m, f32 spot_outer, u32 size)`: the mip for a spot sample from the pixel's world footprint, the distance and the cone, clamped to the chain, `log2(max(footprint / (2 * distance * tan(outer) / size), 1))`; `cookie_lod_sun(f32 pixel_footprint_m, f32 size_m, u32 texels)` the same for the sun's tiling.
  - `struct CookiePlan { u32 layer_of[kMaxCookieLayers]; u32 key[kMaxCookieLayers]; u32 count; }` and `CookiePlan plan_cookies(Span<const PointLight>, const DirectionalLight&, u32 max_layers, u32 max_lights, Span<u32> light_record)`: walks the lights in submission order, drops lights beyond `max_lights` cookied lights (the sun's cookie is not counted against it and takes a layer first), gives each distinct key one layer in first-seen order (stable across frames, so an unchanged scene bakes nothing), and writes each cookied light's record index (or `~0u`) into `light_record`; keys beyond `max_layers` leave their lights un-cookied and count into the drop counter.
  - `struct GpuCookieRecord { math::mat4 view_proj; math::vec4 params; };` (80 bytes; `params.x` layer, `.y` strength, `.z` edge, `.w` unused) and `GpuCookieRecord cookie_record(const PointLight&, u32 layer)`.
  - `inline constexpr u32 kMaxCookieLayers = 16;`, `kMaxCookieLights = 16;`.
- **Binding (the load-bearing decision; checked 2026-11-04).** The lit mesh and terrain fragment shader (`kMeshFS`, `kTerrainFS`, one `MCE_GLSL_MESH_FS` body) already samples all 16 sampler banks: decal textures 0 to 2, SSR 3, volumetrics 4, irradiance volume 5, albedo 6, normal 7, ORM 8, shadow cascades and the local-shadow atlas 9, sky 10, SSAO 11, pre-filtered sky 12, emissive 13, BRDF LUT 14, SH9 15 (`rhi/types.h` `bank::`, `shaders3d.h`), and the Metal and WebGPU backends are hard-capped at 16 (`kMaxTextureSlots`, `shader_wgsl.h`). There is no free bank. The per-material maps (6, 7, 8, 13) are arrays bound per draw (`TextureStore` keys, layer 0), and the decals' banks 0 to 2 are bound once per frame for at most `kMaxDecalTextures` = 3 distinct keys. The cookie atlas therefore **shares bank 2 with decal texture slot 2**:

  - The atlas is a `sampler2DArray` (one layer per distinct cookie texture, `RGBA8_UNorm`, full mip chain, `REPEAT` wrap so the sun tiles; the spot path clamps the uv and zeroes outside [0, 1] by its own inside test, so the wrap mode does not matter for it). It is bound at `bank::kDecalTexture0 + 2` and declared once in GLSL as `u_slot2` (the existing `u_decal2` renamed; `decal_texture()` and `cookie_sample()` both read it), because two variables on one binding do not compile on the Vulkan path.
  - A frame that has a cookie caps its decal textures at **two**, and decals that would have needed the third texture are **skipped** (not drawn), with a one-time warning of their own ("a frame with light cookies draws decals from at most 2 distinct textures"). *Amended in 40c:* the plan had `Scene3D::build` stop at `kMaxDecalTextures - 1` and draw those decals untextured through the slot -1 path; that needs `scene3d.h`, frozen after 40a, so the cap is in `Forward3D::bind_decals` (`forward3d_scale.cpp`), which skips them because their slot is already assigned by the build. A frame without a cookie keeps three decal textures and binds the decal texture as today, so the off frame is bit-identical. The two features share the bank per frame, never inside one draw's data.
  - The records are a std430 buffer `LightCookies` at a new storage bank `kLightCookies` in `rhi/types.h` (storage banks are their own namespace; 40a picks the number, the free ones below 4 are the candidates, and checks the SDL GPU storage-buffer slot count and the Metal and WebGPU maps), bound with `set_storage_data` like `kLocalShadows` (a one-record dummy when none, as `kNoShadow`). The per-light link is `Light.shadow.z` = record index + 1 (0 = no cookie; floats hold the integers exactly) and `Light.shadow.w` is free for later. The sun needs no record buffer: its layer, strength, size, roll and offset ride in two new `vec4` members of `PassU` (`p_sun_cookie`: layer, strength, size, roll; `p_sun_cookie_off`: offset xy, tiles on), added to the GLSL block in `shaders3d.h` and the C++ mirror in `forward3d.cpp` with the existing `static_assert`s (neither is a wide header).
  - `p_local.z` = cookies on (the frame-level switch the shader tests first), mirroring `p_local.x` for local shadows.

  The alternatives rejected, recorded because 40c re-verifies the first one against the code before writing anything: (1) a new 17th bank is impossible on Metal and WebGPU; (2) freeing a bank by moving the SH9 irradiance (bank 15, nine texels) into `PassU` is a larger change to every lit variant and to `PassU`, for a feature that is opt-in; it stays a follow-up if more per-frame textures are wanted; (3) one cookie texture per light bound like the decals would need up to 16 banks; (4) putting the cookies into the local-shadow atlas (bank 9) fails because that array is a depth-compare `sampler2DArrayShadow`.
- **Atlas bake (render, 40c).** `record_cookie_bake` runs once per frame before the shadow passes (so a frame's first pass sees a complete atlas), only when `plan_cookies` returns layers whose key or `TextureStore` generation changed since the last bake: for each changed layer it draws one fullscreen triangle sampling the `TextureStore` texture (bilinear, any source size) into mip 0 of the layer at the tier's cookie size, then the mip chain by successive 2x box passes (the IBL pre-filter chain is the precedent for render-to-mip). A texture that is not loaded yet bakes as white (no pattern) and is retried each frame until it exists. The atlas is created on first use and recreated when the tier's size or layer count changes; the layers are never read back. Its time falls in the existing shadow bucket's timestamp; no new timestamp, no header change beyond 40a. If `RenderPassDesc` cannot target a layer and a mip of an array (40c checks; local shadows target layers, mips are the question), the fallback is a bake into a per-size scratch target followed by `update_texture` from a readback-free path; if neither exists the milestone stops at 40c and the finding goes in this record (the one real unknown of the plan).
  - **Deviation (40c, 2026-11-04; accepted by the independent review and the owner, the routine 2 thread, in place of the stop condition).** Neither exists: `rhi::PassAttachment` has `layer` and no `mip`, the RHI has no texture copy or generate-mips, the IBL chain renders cube faces (layers), not mips, and `update_texture` takes CPU data only. The atlas is therefore one hardware mip with the chain **packed** in each layer: a layer is 1.5S x S, level 0 at (0, 0), level k >= 1 of S/2^k at (S, S - 2 S/2^k) (rows from v = 0), and each level is its own render pass (Clear for level 0, Load after) with a viewport over its square, one fullscreen draw from the source texture (four taps at the next finer source mip when minifying, one bilinear tap otherwise; no level reads another, no scratch target). The lit shader filters trilinearly by hand: two `textureLod(..., 0)` fetches, each clamped half a texel inside its level, mixed by the fractional lod. Costs: 1.5x the memory of a plain layer (Memory below), log2(S) + 1 render passes per baked layer (10 at High), one more fetch than a hardware trilinear.
  - **Consequence for 40e (decision point).** `REPEAT` cannot tile a packed level: the hardware wrap is over the whole 1.5S x S layer. The sun cookie must pick (a) a wrapped gutter around each packed level, baked with the source's repeat and no clamp, the lit fetch then wrapping uv inside the level before the half-texel clamp, or (b) a manual wrapped bilinear (four `texelFetch` per level, eight per trilinear sample). *Decided in 40e: (b)*, with the numbers in the 40e row of Step status: it keeps the packing, the spot bake and the spot shader as they are (spot frames and `cookie_spot_window` byte-identical), costs no memory, and costs the sun four point loads per level where (a) would issue one bilinear fetch (the same eight texels read either way).
- **Lit-pass sampling (render, 40d).** In the local-light loop of `MCE_GLSL_MESH_FS`, after the cone attenuation and `if (att <= 0.0) continue;` and **before** the `local_shadow` block:

  ```
  if (p_local.z > 0.5 && L.shadow.z > 0.0) {
      CookieRec c = l_cookies[int(L.shadow.z) - 1];
      vec4 q = c.vp * vec4(v_world, 1.0);
      vec3 tint = vec3(0.0);
      if (q.w > 0.0) {
          vec2 uv = q.xy / q.w * 0.5 + 0.5;
          if (all(greaterThanEqual(uv, vec2(0.0))) && all(lessThanEqual(uv, vec2(1.0))))
              tint = textureLod(u_slot2, vec3(uv, c.params.x), cookie_lod(...)).rgb * cookie_edge(uv, c.params.z);
      }
      lc = mix(vec3(1.0), tint, c.params.y);   // multiplies the light colour below
      if (max(lc.r, max(lc.g, lc.b)) <= 0.0) continue;  // a black texel skips the shadow taps
  }
  ```

  `lc` multiplies `L.color.rgb * att` in the `shade` call (default `vec3(1.0)`, so a non-cookied light computes the same value; the `shade` argument for a non-cookied light is the expression as today). The LOD uses a pixel footprint computed **once before the loop** (`px = length(dFdx(v_world)) + length(dFdy(v_world))`, in uniform control flow, as the decals do), because derivatives inside the loop's divergent branches are undefined; the sample is `textureLod`, never an implicit-derivative `texture`. Cost per cookied light in the pixel's cluster: one `mat4 * vec4`, one record fetch, one trilinear fetch; a non-cookied light pays one float compare. The terrain pass shares the body, so it gets the cookie with no extra work; the mesh and terrain variants that define their own loops (none today: `l_indices[range.x + i]` appears in `kMeshFS` and `kVolInjectFS` only) are re-grepped in 40d.
- **Sun term (render, 40e).** `shade(n, v, l, ..., p_light_color.rgb) * sh` becomes `... * sh * sun_cookie()` where `sun_cookie()` is 1 when `p_local.z` is 0 or the sun has no cookie, else `mix(vec3(1), textureLod(u_slot2, vec3(uv, layer), lod).rgb, strength)` with `uv = sun_cookie_uv(v_world, ...)` (a vec3, so the sun can be tinted). It multiplies the direct sun term only, **after** the cascade shadow, the contact shadow and the n·l clamp, never the ambient, the IBL, the volume's sun scatter inside `Volumetrics` or the sky; a sun cookie plus a sun shadow is a product. The same `p_sun_cookie` feeds `kVolInjectFS` in 40f.
- **Volumetric injection (render, 40f).** `kVolInjectFS` loops the same `Light` records (`l_lights[l_indices[range.x + i]]`) and the sun. It includes `MCE_GLSL_LIGHTS` but not the mesh body, so the cookie sampling is factored into a small `MCE_GLSL_COOKIE` macro (the records buffer, `cookie_lod`, `cookie_edge`, the spot and sun samplers) included by `kMeshFS`, `kTerrainFS` and `kVolInjectFS`. In the pass the atlas is bound at a bank the inject shader has free (the pass has no decals), and the LOD is the froxel's footprint. With `Volumetrics` off the pass does not run and nothing is spent. This is what makes a window-grid spot draw a lattice of shafts.
- **Quality tiers (render, 40b).** Cookies are art direction, so no tier removes them; the tier only bounds the cost: `QualitySettings::light_cookies` (true on every tier), `light_cookie_size` (Low 128, Medium 256, High 512), `light_cookie_layers` (Low 4, Medium 8, High 16) and `light_cookie_lights` (Low 4, Medium 8, High 16, the per-frame count of cookied lights, chosen by screen importance the way `LocalShadowSettings::max_lights` is: nearest and biggest first, ties by submission order). Cvar `r.light_cookies` (bool, Saved, "Light cookies (a texture projected by spot lights and the sun; off = plain lights)") next to `r.lens_flare` in `quality.cpp`. The resolve (`plan_cookies` in `Forward3D::record_view`) and `Kit3DExtract` (which writes `cookie = ~0u` / `SunCookie{}` when the tier or the cvar says off, so a stale one never survives, the 38h pattern) both apply them; `quality.h` (122 TUs, under the warning) only gains the four fields.
- **Kit components (kit, 40g).** Opt-in, in a new header `mce/kit/light_cookie.h` so the wide `kit3d.h` (246 TUs) and `weather.h` are not touched (`SpotLight3D` keeps its layout; a spot with a `LightCookie3D` on the same entity is the pairing, the way `LightFlicker` pairs with a light):
  - `LightCookie3D`: `asset::AssetRef<asset::Texture> texture` (as `Decal3D`), `strength` 1 (`Range{0, 1}`), `roll_deg` 0 (`Range{-180, 180}`), `roll_speed_deg` 0 (`Range{-360, 360}`, degrees per second: a slowly turning gobo), `edge` 0 (`Range{0, 1}`), `enabled` 1, plus an explicit zeroed `pad_`. It is read by the `SpotLight3D` extract loop through a `world.try_get<const LightCookie3D>(e)` (one extra lookup per visible spot light), which fills `PointLight::cookie*`; a cookie on a `PointLight3D` is ignored with the Help text saying so.
  - `SunCookie3D`: one per scene (the first in ECS order is read, like `LensFlare3D`), `texture`, `strength`, `size`, `offset` (vec2), `scroll` (vec2, metres per second), `roll_deg`, `enabled`, `pad_`. `Kit3DExtract` writes `DirectionalLight::cookie` from it before `scene.set_light(...)`.
  - The texture key comes from the extract's existing `texture_key(guid)`, so a cookie that is missing, unloaded or not a texture asset gives `~0u` (no cookie, the plain light) and a one-time warning; nothing throws.
  - Both are plain POD columns with `Save` fields, registered by `install_kit3d` next to `LensFlare3D`, appear in the editor inspector and the MCP component tools without further code (the `capabilities` table row, the three `engine_components.cpp` includes and the transcript golden `components` bump are 40g's checklist, as in 39d), and are cooked with the scene.
- **Reach: Luau and MCP.** A Luau API is out of scope as a dedicated module; scripts and agents reach cookies the way they reach every kit component: Luau through the generic component access (`world:get_component` / `set_component` on `LightCookie3D` and `SunCookie3D` by name, the reflected fields), MCP through the existing entity and component tools (`add_component`, `set_component`, `get_component`), so an agent can put a window grid on a lamp and read it back. 40g's tests exercise one set and one get through the reflection path; 40i writes the user-guide paragraph with the two examples. A one-off cookie in `render_frame` is the same follow-up as the outline's `environment` override.
- **Determinism.** The cookie is a pure function of the light, the cookie texture's pixels and the camera; it never feeds the simulation, a component or the state hash. `roll_speed_deg` and `scroll` advance in the extract from the frame clock (a render-side value), not from a tick-hashed component: the stored fields are the authored base values, never the animated ones, so a save and a state hash do not depend on the clock. A scene without a cookie component is byte-identical; a scene with one differs only in the saved component and in frames.

## Cost and budget

- **GPU work when on.** Bake: per changed layer one fullscreen draw into mip 0 plus the mip chain (about 1.33 times the layer's texels written), only when a key or generation changes, so a static scene bakes once; at High 512x512x16 layers that is about 21 MB of RGBA8 writes at most, once. Per frame: the lit pass pays, per pixel, per cookied light in its cluster, one `mat4 * vec4`, a record fetch and one trilinear RGBA8 fetch (a cache-friendly 512x512 mip chain), and saves the nine local-shadow taps wherever the cookie is black (window shadows, flashlight mask). The sun cookie is one more fetch per lit pixel. Targets on a mid GPU at 1080p High: at most 0.15 ms for four cookied lights over a typical interior view and 0.1 ms for the sun cookie over the frame (both measured on the GPU list, not here). Volumetric injection pays the same per froxel per cookied light, in the existing injection pass.
- **Memory.** The atlas (packed, 1.5S x S per layer, see Atlas bake): Low 192x128x4 (about 0.4 MB), Medium 384x256x8 (about 3.1 MB), High 768x512x16 (about 25 MB), RGBA8, created only once a cookie is in the frame; the records buffer is 80 bytes per cookied light plus a dummy.
- **CPU.** One `plan_cookies` per view and frame over the point lights (a pass over an array that the cluster build already walks, no allocation: fixed arrays, `kMaxCookieLights` records), one `cookie_record` per cookied light (a `look_at` and a perspective, a few dozen flops); the extract does one extra component lookup per visible spot light.
- **Off.** Nothing: no atlas, no bake draw, no records upload beyond the dummy `set_storage_data` the existing local-shadow path already does for its own dummy, one frame-level float compare in the shader. The bank 2 binding is the decal texture exactly as today.

## Risks

- **No free sampler bank.** The decisive constraint (Design, Binding). Sharing bank 2 costs a frame with a cookie its third decal texture. Accepted for an opt-in feature and recorded in the user guide; the recovery if it hurts is freeing a bank by folding the SH9 texture into `PassU` (follow-up). 40c re-verifies the claim first, because everything else stacks on it; if a bank turns out to be free, the sharing and the two-decal cap are dropped from 40c and the rest of the plan is unchanged.
- **Render-to-layer-and-mip unknown.** The bake needs a render target that is one mip of one array layer. Local shadows target layers; mips as targets are the question (40c checks `RenderPassDesc` and the three non-GL backends). The fallback and the stop condition are in Design, Atlas bake. *Resolved in 40c:* not supported; the mips are packed (Design, Atlas bake, Deviation).
- **Views with different cookie sets re-bake.** The atlas is one per renderer and its layers follow the plan of the view that baked last, so two views of a frame whose scenes have different cookie textures (or the same textures in a different first-seen order) re-bake the changed layers in every view of every frame. Correct (each view checks its own tags), but the bake cost recurs; a per-view layer map or a key-sorted plan is a follow-up if a split-screen game hits it.
- **Texture generation and streaming.** A cookie texture that streams in late bakes as white (no pattern) until it exists, so a light may flash from plain to shaped; the key and generation cache re-bakes on change. Documented; a hold-until-loaded option is a follow-up.
- **Derivatives and the loop.** The footprint must be computed outside the divergent loop; a derivative inside is undefined and shows as shimmering mip selection on some drivers only. The 40d review checks it first, and the GPU list covers it on real hardware.
- **Cookie plus shadow cost balance.** The cookie before the shadow saves shadow taps where it is black but adds a fetch where it is bright; for a nearly-all-bright cookie the net cost is a little worse than a plain light. Accepted: `cookie_strength` 0 and an absent cookie component both cost nothing.
- **Colour and exposure.** A dark cookie lowers the lit area's brightness and auto exposure compensates; a cookie is multiplied in linear light (the PNGs are decoded as sRGB, so a 50 percent grey texel is about 0.21 linear). 40h's generator writes the intended values and the user guide says the cookie is a linear multiplier of the light.
- **Sun plane swim and tiling seams.** *Since the 40e review* the pattern is projected onto the ground plane along the sun, so on flat ground it does not move with the sun at all; on a raised or sloped surface it shifts as the sun moves (the shadow of a fixed canopy would do the same), and a sun lower than about 2.9 degrees is projected as if at that height (`kSunCookieMinDy`). Tiling uses `REPEAT` with mips, so a texture that is not seamless shows a seam; documented, not fixed.
- **Look.** The default strength, the sun size and the quality of a 128-texel Low cookie are look choices that only a real display can judge; llvmpipe pixels prove placement, the off-checks and stability, not taste.
