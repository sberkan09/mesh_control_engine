# M-GI2 — Irradiance volume, second pass: probes that see walls, probes that leave them, a volume that follows the camera (milestone plan)

**Status:** planned 2026-11-09 (plan only, nothing built). Steps 50a to 50i, in seven PRs (50a, this plan; 50b alone, the seam; 50c+50d; 50e; 50f+50g; 50h; 50i). The only wide-header edits are in 50b: `scene3d.h` (the carrier fields of `render::GiVolume` and the probe layout in `gi_volume_size`) and `forward3d.h` (four stats fields, one forward-declared state pointer, one defaulted `draw_meshes` parameter), shipped together as the seam PR, merged first. **Owner: the M-VISUAL routine 2 thread.** Follow-up of M-VISUAL step 6 (the irradiance volume; its "Limits" paragraph in [M-VISUAL.md](M-VISUAL.md) says "Light leaks through walls thinner than the probe spacing, and probes buried in walls darken what is near them (no probe relocation or visibility term yet)"), of [M-OPENWORLD-LIGHT](M-OPENWORLD-LIGHT.md) (which put one `GiVolume3D` of 16 x 4 x 16 probes over the middle 240 x 240 m of `samples/09_openworld`) and of the `render::GiVolume` comment in `scene3d.h` ("Probes buried in walls see their backfaces and darken what is near them, so keep the grid's planes off the walls"). Structure and rules follow [M-WATER2](M-WATER2.md) and [M-REFLECT2](M-REFLECT2.md). No Linear issues (free limit); this record is the tracker.

**Naming:** the volume is **not rebuilt**. `render::GiVolume` (`scene3d.h`), `kit::GiVolume3D` (`kit3d.h`), `Forward3D::record_gi` / `gi_capture` / `record_gi_export`, the shaders `kGiProjectFS` / `kGiEncodeFS` and the lookup `gi_irradiance2` / `gi_irradiance` (`shaders3d.h`), the bank `rhi::bank::kGiVolume` = 5, the cvar `r.gi`, the `.mcenv` bake and the editor's `EditorSession::bake_gi_volume` all exist and stay. On 2026-11-09 `git grep -niE 'gi2\b|M-GI2|gi_vis|givis|gi_visibility|GiVisibility|gi_relocat|gi_follow|gi_moments|gi_texels|r\.gi\.'` over `src`, `tests`, `samples`, `docs` and `scripts` finds nothing but two binary images (checked on `origin/master` 0d1988ed). `follow_camera` exists as a field of `kit::Precipitation3D` (`mce/kit/precipitation.h`, "1: sit `height` above the primary camera"): a different component, and the new kit field below reuses the name on purpose, with the same meaning (follow the primary camera). `relocate` exists only as a private method of `Array` (`mce/containers/array.h`), unrelated. New names: the carrier fields `GiVolume::visibility`, `relocate`, `max_offset` and `follow`; the inline `gi_texels_per_probe`; the stats `Forward3DStats::gi_visibility`, `gi_relocated`, `gi_scrolled`, `gi_vis_draws`; the narrow render header `mce/render/gi_probes.h` (`src/gi_probes.cpp`, holding the pure helpers and `GiProbeState`); the shader `kGiVisFS`; the cvar `r.gi.visibility`; the kit component `GiVisibility3D` in its own narrow header `mce/kit/gi_visibility.h`; the golden `gi_thin_wall`.

**Goal.** Make the irradiance volume trustworthy next to walls, so a scene no longer has to keep "the grid's planes off the walls" by hand: (1) a surface is lit only by the probes that can **see** it: a probe on the far side of a wall (thinner than the probe spacing) or under the ground stops leaking its light through, through a per-probe distance record and a Chebyshev visibility weight in `gi_irradiance2` (the DDGI weight, without ray tracing: the record is captured by rasterisation like the light already is); (2) a probe that sits **inside** geometry (it sees mostly back faces) is classified off and weighs nothing, and on request a probe a little way inside a solid is **relocated** out of it, up to a bounded offset; (3) a volume can **follow the camera** in steps of whole cells, keeping every probe it already captured (toroidal addressing) and capturing only the slab that scrolled in, so `09_openworld` can put 32 x 8 x 32 probes (or what 50c's measured capture budget allows) around the player instead of 16 x 4 x 16 over a fixed 240 m square with 16 m between probes and its bottom layer under the street. All of it is opt-in through one new kit component; a scene without it renders byte-identically, every golden stays, nothing is hashed.

**Not in scope:** ray-traced or compute-traced probes (the engine's captures are rasterised and stay so), per-probe octahedral depth maps (DDGI's 16 x 16 visibility texels per probe; this plan stores a six-lobe moment record, follow-up 2), specular GI or probe reflections, more than one `GiVolume3D` (M-VISUAL3's "out" list: one volume texture at bank 5; the main pass's 16 fragment sampler slots are all taken), cascaded or nested volumes (follow-up 5), vertical following (the follow is on x and z; y stays the authored band), moving occluders in the visibility record (characters, cars and props are left out of it on purpose; follow-up 6), GPU-side relocation (needs an asynchronous readback the RHI does not have; follow-up 3), a headless or MCP bake command and a committed `09_openworld` bake (follow-up 1: the Low tier of the city therefore keeps drawing without GI, as today), a change to `QualitySettings` (`quality.h`), `Environment3D` or `kit::GiVolume3D` (the visibility settings are a separate component, see Design 5), a change to the editor's bake code beyond what follows from a wider volume texture, new PassU lanes, a new sampler bank, Metal and WebGPU verification (the shader text is shared; it goes to the GPU list).

## Investigation (what the code does today; the plan below rests on these facts)

Checked on `origin/master` 0d1988ed, 2026-11-09. Line numbers are approximate.

- **The carrier.** `render::GiVolume` (`scene3d.h` about 414 to 447): `enabled`, `bounds` (world box), `counts[3]` (probes per axis, clamped to 2..`kMaxGiProbesPerAxis` = 32 by `Scene3D::set_gi_volume`), `intensity`, `normal_bias` (0.25 m), `fade_distance` (1 m), `hysteresis` (0.85), `probes_per_frame` (16), `version`, `baked` (a `TextureStore` key, ~0u = none), `updates` (false on the Low tier). One volume per scene. `gi_volume_size(gi)` is `{counts[0] * 4, counts[1] * counts[2]}`: **4 texels per probe** along x (probe x-major), one row per (y, z) pair.
- **The texture.** `Forward3D::gi_volume_` is RGBA16F of that size (`forward3d.h` about 308 to 327, `record_gi` in `forward3d.cpp` about 2863). Each probe holds a diffuse L1 lobe as **four tetrahedral samples** (`kGiTetra`), `V_k` = the sphere integral of `L (1 + w.d_k) / 4 pi`. The main pass reads it at `rhi::bank::kGiVolume` = 5 (`types.h`) with `texelFetch`, point sampler. The alpha channel is **not storage**: `gi_project_` is created with `BlendMode::Alpha` and `kGiProjectFS` writes `alpha = the new value's weight` (1 on the fill, `1 - hysteresis` on rolling updates), so blending keeps the rest of the old value; only rgb is meaningful.
- **The capture.** `gi_capture` (`forward3d.cpp` about 2933 to 3145): per probe six 90-degree faces of `kGiFaceSize` = 16 px (`probe_face_views`, +x -x +y -y +z -z, the **camera's** near and far planes) into `gi_faces_` (RGBA16F 2D array, `6 * kGiMaxBatchProbes` = 192 layers) with a D32 depth array whose store is `DontCare`. Each face is lit like the main pass (the sun through its own shadow fit over the volume's bounding sphere, up to `kGiMaxCaptureLights` = 32 point and spot lights ranked for the probe, emissive surfaces, the sky behind, the volume itself for the next bounce), draws at most `kGiMaxFaceDraws` = 96 of `scene.probe_draws()` (opaque, not alpha-blended; the widest first), clears to the fog colour with alpha 1. A frame's captures stop at `kGiCaptureBudget` = 1024 uniform uploads (a draw is one, a face three; the first probe always goes). The fill captures `kGiMaxBatchProbes` = 32 probes a frame and the volume lights the frame once full; afterwards `min(probes_per_frame, 32)` probes are re-captured per frame (x-major, wrapping). `kGiProjectFS` then projects the faces of a run of probes along one volume row into their 4 texels (a pass per run, the viewport selects the texels).
- **Back faces are not drawn.** The captures go through `draw_meshes(..., main = false)`, which picks the main pass's pipelines (`forward3d.cpp` about 3285): single-sided opaque materials use `mesh_cull_` / `skinned_cull_` with `CullMode::Back` (about 1104), only `DoubleSided` materials use the `CullMode::None` twins. So a probe **inside a closed single-sided shell sees through it** (the shell's faces are all back faces from inside): it records the light outdoors, as if the shell were not there, and hands it to the surfaces around it. Only thick two-sided walls give the "darkening" the comment names. Either way the probe's light belongs to a place nobody stands in. A capture that should detect "inside geometry" must draw back faces and know which faces were back faces.
- **The lookup.** `gi_irradiance2(p, n, out back)` (`shaders3d.h` about 851 to 887, inside `MCE_GLSL_MESH_FS`): fade by the distance outside the box, `g = (clamp(p + n * normal_bias) - bmin) / cell`, the 8 probes of the cell by **trilinear weight times a smooth backface term** `b = (dot(normalize(probe - p), n) + 1) / 2`, `w *= b * b + 0.05` (the 0.05 floor: a probe behind the surface still counts), 4 `texelFetch` per probe (32 a pixel), the front and back L1 sums. `kMeshFS` uses it for the diffuse ambient, the sky-visibility factor of the reflections, the translucent back side (`thin_amb`) and the sheen (`shaders3d.h` about 1334 to 1380). There is **no distance or visibility information** anywhere in the volume.
- **The lanes.** `Forward3DPassU` (`forward3d.cpp` about 104 to 108, `MCE_GLSL_PASS_U` lines 51 to 54): `p_gi` (on, intensity, normal bias, fade distance), `p_gi_min.xyz`, `p_gi_max.xyz`, `p_gi_counts.xyz`. `bind_ambient` fills `gi_counts.w = 0`, `gi_min.w = 0`, `gi_max.w = 0`, and no shader reads them: **three free scalars** in lanes the GI already owns. PassU is 1536 bytes with **3 free vec4** (`free_[3]`, after M-WATER2's `p_water6`).
- **The lit shader is full.** `kMeshFS` is **65 295 bytes** and `kTerrainFS` (the same text) **65 317 bytes** (measured with `strlen` on a host build of `shaders3d.h`, 2026-11-09), against 65 535, the longest literal MSVC accepts after concatenation and clang's `-Woverlength-strings` bound: **240 and 218 bytes of room**. M-TRANSLUCENT 41d hit the limit before and made room by moving GLSL comments into C++ comments (`docs/milestones/M-TRANSLUCENT.md`, "Literal length"); its record asks the next lit feature to do the same. The GLSL comments still inside `kMeshFS` add up to about **16 KB** (a `//`-to-end-of-line count over the concatenated literal), so the room exists, but it must be made first. `kGiProjectFS` is 2 026 bytes, `kGiEncodeFS` 490.
- **No free sampler, no async readback.** All 16 fragment sampler slots of the main pass are taken (M-VISUAL3, "Fragment samplers of the main pass are full"): any new per-probe data must live **in the volume texture itself** (more texels per probe), not in a second texture. `RenderDevice::read_pixels` / `read_buffer` stall the GPU and are "test and tooling plumbing": the runtime cannot read capture results back to the CPU, so anything the CPU needs (where to place a probe's capture) must be computed on the CPU.
- **Bake and Low tier.** `record_gi_export` encodes the whole volume texel for texel as RGBM RGBA8 (`kGiEncodeFS`, range `kGiRgbmRange` = 16); `EditorSession::bake_gi_volume` (`editor_session.cpp` about 149) writes it as a one-layer `.mcenv` under `assets/gi/<scene>/` after `PreviewRenderer::bake_gi` filled the volume and re-captured every probe twice (three bounces). `record_gi` accepts a bake only when its size equals `gi_volume_size(gi)` (otherwise a warning and the runtime fill) and decodes it through `kProbeDecodeFS`. `apply_quality(q, GiVolume&)` (`quality.cpp` about 300) clears `updates` on Low (`q.gi_updates`): Low uses the bake only, and without one draws without GI. RGBM keeps about 8 bits of relative precision for any value in 0..16, so values normalised to 0..1 survive the bake.
- **Keys and the floating origin.** `gi_key` (`forward3d.cpp` about 967) hashes the bounds **plus `origin_`** (f64), the counts, the version and the bake handle: a rebase leaves a fixed volume's key (and its probes) alone, any change of the world box refills every probe. A camera-following box would refill on every move today.
- **The kit side.** `kit::GiVolume3D` (`kit3d.h` about 98 to 130, `static_assert(sizeof(GiVolume3D) == 88)` in `kit3d.cpp`): `half_extents`, `offset`, `probes_x/y/z` (Range 2..32), `intensity`, `normal_bias`, `fade_distance`, `hysteresis`, `probes_per_frame`, `version`, `enabled`, `baked`. `Kit3DExtract` (`kit3d_extract.cpp` about 1782) maps it onto the carrier, forces hysteresis 0 and 32 probes a frame in bake mode, applies the quality tier, and keeps meshes and lights within the box plus its half diagonal in the scene off-screen (`gi_keep`, `gi_reach`) so captures see the whole room. Components are hashed by their bytes (`World::state_hash`), so new fields on `GiVolume3D` would change the state hash and the saved bytes of every scene with a volume.
- **The city.** `samples/09_openworld/gen_assets.py` line 1365 adds the volume last: centre (0, 12, 0), `half_extents` (120, 14, 120), 16 x 4 x 16 probes, `probes_per_frame` 32. So the probes are **16 m apart on x and z** (240 / 15; not "about 15 m") and 9.33 m apart on y, at y = -2, 7.33, 16.67 and 26. The ground is a 0.5 m slab whose top is at y = 0 (`Ground mesh` at y -0.25, line 702): **256 of the 1024 probes (the whole bottom layer) sit 2 m below the street**, 1.5 m under the slab's underside. With today's weights, a street point in the middle of a cell (11.3 m from the four probe columns) takes **about 52 %** of its GI weight from those buried probes (trilinear y weight 0.76 times backface term 0.22, against 0.24 times 0.65 for the layer above), and a point right above a probe column still about 13 %. ## Design

### 1. What a probe records: a six-lobe distance record and a state, in the same texture (50c, 50d)

Options weighed:

| Option | Verdict |
|---|---|
| A. A second texture of per-probe depth (DDGI's octahedral 16 x 16 per probe) | **No.** No free sampler bank in the main pass (Investigation); 64 x more texels than the volume holds today; the lookup would sample a filtered octahedral map, 8 more taps a pixel at least. Follow-up 2 if the coarse record below proves too coarse. |
| B. One mean distance and one mean square per probe (2 more texels, isotropic) | **No.** Chebyshev visibility needs the distance **in the direction of the shading point**. An isotropic mean cannot tell the wall on one side of a probe from the open street on the other: it would dim the probe for every surface or for none. |
| C. Write the radial distance into the lit capture's alpha (`kMeshFS` in a capture mode) and draw captures two-sided | **No.** Costs bytes in a lit shader with 240 bytes of room; drawing the lit capture two-sided changes the light of every visibility volume's probes (the inside of a shell becomes a lit, shadowed surface), so "visibility only changes weights" could no longer be tested; alpha also carries `base.a` for blended pipelines. |
| **D. A separate geometry-only "vis" pass per face (`kGiVisFS`, back faces drawn, signed distance), projected into 6 more texels per probe** | **Chosen.** The lit faces stay exactly as today; the vis pass is a tiny fragment shader on the mesh vertex stage; it runs only when the volume asks for visibility and only on the fill, a refill and a scrolled-in slab (not on rolling light updates), so the steady-state cost is unchanged. |

- **The vis pass.** For each face of a probe being filled, after the lit face: a pass into `gi_vis_faces_` (a second RGBA16F 2D array of 16 x 16 x 192 layers, held in `GiProbeState`, so no `forward3d.h` member; one array of 384 layers would pass the 256 layers Vulkan and WebGPU guarantee) reusing the face's depth layer (cleared again), drawing the same visible list through the **prepass path** of `draw_meshes` with two new pipelines (`kGiVisFS` over `kMeshVS`, depth-tested, `CullMode::None`; the seam's `draw_meshes` parameter swaps them in for `ao_prepass_` / `ao_prepass_skinned_`). `kGiVisFS` (about 450 bytes, `kAoPrepassFS`'s alpha-mask discard plus one line) writes `r = length(v_world - u_camera_pos.xyz)`, negated when `!gl_FrontFacing`; the clear is `+kGiVisFar` (nothing hit). **What is drawn:** rigid draws whose bounding radius is at least `kGiVisMinRadius` (start 0.5) times the probe spacing; skinned draws never. Walls, floors, buildings and terrain occlude; characters, cars, props and debris do not, so a car parked next to a probe at fill time does not leave a stale shadow in the record (moving occluders are follow-up 6). The cut is a constant in `gi_probes.h`, tuned in 50d against the city audit.
- **The record (texels 4 to 9 of a probe).** The volume texture gets `T` texels per probe: `T = 4` (today) or `T = 10` when the volume asks for visibility, relocation or following (`gi_texels_per_probe`, 50b; `gi_volume_size` becomes `{counts[0] * T, counts[1] * counts[2]}`, unchanged for every existing volume). `kGiProjectFS` reads the vis faces too and writes, normalised to 0..1 by `dmax = kGiVisRangeCells * cell diagonal` (start 1.5; distances are clamped to `dmax`, which also keeps the squares small):
  - texels 4 and 5: the mean distance `mu` of the hits (absolute values) in a cosine-power lobe (exponent `kGiVisLobePower`, start 4) around +x, +y, +z (texel 4 rgb) and -x, -y, -z (texel 5 rgb);
  - texels 6 and 7: the mean square `m2` in the same lobes;
  - texel 8: the relocation offset (Design 3), `0.5 + 0.5 * offset / (max_offset * spacing)` per axis; 0.5 = none;
  - texel 9: r = the **back-face share** (the solid-angle share of vis texels with a negative distance), set to 1 for a probe that scrolled in and was not captured yet (Design 4); g, b = 0.
  Texels 4 to 9 are written with alpha 1 on the fill, a refill and a slab capture, and with **alpha 0 on rolling light updates**, which leaves them bit for bit (`src * 0 + dst * 1`). Only rgb is meaningful, as for texels 0 to 3.
- **Reading a direction from six lobes.** For a unit direction `d` the record gives `mu(d) = sum_i d_i^2 * mu[sign(d_i), i]` and the same for `m2` (ambient-cube interpolation): 4 more `texelFetch` per probe. The CPU twin `gi_record_at` (`gi_probes.h`) pins the formula; a GL test compares the shader against it through a probe placed in a known box.
- **Bake.** Every texel of the record is in 0..1, which the existing RGBM export and `kProbeDecodeFS` carry at about 8 bits: **no new encode or decode shader, no new `.mcenv` format**. The bake's width names its layout: a bake made before the volume asked for visibility (4 texels a probe) no longer matches `gi_volume_size` and is refused with today's warning (runtime fill; on Low, no GI until re-baked). Said in the guide. The Low tier, which captures nothing, gets visibility from the bake's record.

### 2. Who sees whom: Chebyshev visibility and classification in `gi_irradiance2` (50e)

Options weighed:

| Option | Verdict |
|---|---|
| A. Keep the backface term, raise its exponent | **No.** It knows the surface normal, not what lies between the probe and the point: a probe on the far side of a 0.2 m wall is "in front" of the floor near the wall and keeps leaking. |
| B. Screen-space or contact occlusion of the GI term | **No.** Only what is on screen, view-dependent, and SSAO already darkens the ambient by local occlusion. |
| **C. DDGI's weight: Chebyshev's bound on the probe's distance record toward the point, times the classification state** | **Chosen.** One more multiplication per probe, data from Design 1, the established answer to both defects named in M-VISUAL. |

- **The weight.** For each of the 8 probes: `q` = the probe's grid position plus its offset (texel 8, when relocation is on), `v = p_b - q` with `p_b` the normal-biased lookup point (the same point the grid cell is chosen from), `r = |v|`, `(mu, m2) = record(v / r) * dmax`. If `r <= mu` the point is in front of the nearest surface: `vis = 1`; otherwise `var = max(m2 - mu^2, kGiMinVariance)`, `vis = (var / (var + (r - mu)^2))^3` (cubed as DDGI does, which keeps thin-wall bleeding low). The existing trilinear and backface terms stay; `w *= vis`, and weights under `kGiCrush` (0.2) are crushed as DDGI does (`w * w * w / kGiCrush^2`). **Classification:** a probe whose back-face share is at or above `kGiBackfaceOff` (start 0.25, DDGI's threshold) gets `w = 0`. A probe inside a closed lot of the city sees the inside of the walls and the roof (back faces over the whole upper hemisphere: a share near 0.5) and is switched off; a probe under the street sees the slab's underside, a **front** face 1.5 m above it, so it is not classified off but its record says "occluded" for every point on the street (Chebyshev). Both defects are covered.
- **No probe left.** When every one of the 8 weights is 0 (deep inside a building, or in a slab not captured yet), `gi_irradiance2` returns `a = 0` for that point: the sky's ambient applies there, as outside the box. Never "the light of a buried probe".
- **Flags in the lanes the GI owns.** `p_gi_counts.w` holds bit flags: 1 = the 10-texel layout, 2 = the visibility weight and classification, 4 = the relocation offsets. `p_gi_max.w` = `dmax` (m). `p_gi_min.w` = the toroidal slot offset of Design 4 (`ox + 32 oy + 1024 oz`, exact in f32 below 32 768). All three are 0 today (`bind_ambient`), and flags 0 runs today's statements: the lookup branches once on `p_gi_counts.w`, so a volume without the component is byte-identical. The captures read the volume through `bind_ambient` too, so the next bounce sees the same weights. **No new PassU lane** (free vec4 stay 3).
- **Making room in `kMeshFS` (50d's first sub-step).** The visibility code (record fetch, Chebyshev, flags, toroidal index, offset) is estimated at 1.0 to 1.4 KB of GLSL, against 240 bytes of room. Before any GI shader change, 50d moves at least 3 KB of GLSL comments out of `MCE_GLSL_MESH_FS` and the macros spliced into it (the irradiance-volume block first) into C++ comments next to them, as M-TRANSLUCENT 41d did. Comments do not reach the compiled shader, so every golden is byte-identical (pipeline-cache keys, which hash the text, change once). The lengths of `kMeshFS` and `kTerrainFS` go into the as-built entries of 50d and 50e.

### 3. Leaving the wall: relocation within a bounded offset, computed on the CPU (50f)

Options weighed:

| Option | Verdict |
|---|---|
| A. DDGI's relocation on the GPU (move along the closest front-face direction, iterate) | **No.** The capture's view matrices are set by the CPU (`probe_face_views`), and the CPU cannot read the GPU's verdict back without a stall (Investigation). Follow-up 3, with an asynchronous readback. |
| B. Relocate in the editor bake only (which may stall) | **No, not alone.** Then only baked volumes move; the runtime fill (Medium and High, every unbaked scene, the following volume) keeps buried probes. Kept in mind for follow-up 3. |
| C. Use the physics colliders (solid by definition) from the kit | **No.** Render cannot see physics; the extract would have to ray-cast per probe and hand a per-probe array through the carrier (`scene3d.h`) every frame. |
| **D. Push a probe out of the oriented boxes of the large rigid draws (the same draws the vis pass uses), on the CPU, deterministic, opt-in** | **Chosen.** `Scene3D::Draw` carries `model` and `GpuMesh::bounds` (the rest-pose model-space box), so the world OBB of each draw is known on the CPU; a closed building, a rock or a crate is solid at its box. |

- **The rule (`gi_relocate`, `gi_probes.h`).** For a probe's grid position `g`: the large rigid draws whose bounding sphere contains `g`, then whose OBB contains it. If none: offset 0. Otherwise the shortest push through the nearest face of each containing box to `face + kGiRelocateMargin` (0.1 m), combined and re-tested (at most 3 rounds, draws in draw order, ties by index); the result is accepted only if it lies outside every large OBB and each component is within `max_offset * spacing` of `g` (`max_offset` a fraction of the probe spacing, default 0.4, clamped 0..0.45 so a probe never leaves its cell). A probe that cannot be freed keeps offset 0 and is switched off by classification (Design 2). Computed when the volume is filled or refilled and for each scrolled-in slab, never per frame; `stats.gi_relocated` counts the probes that moved.
- **Both sides agree.** The capture uses `g + offset` for `probe_face_views` and the sun-fit's ordering; the projection writes the offset into texel 8 (a `vec4 offsets[32]` array in `GiProjectU`, one entry per probe of the pass's run); the lookup reads it (flag 4). A volume with `relocate` off writes 0.5 there and the lookup skips the fetch.
- **The limit, said plainly.** A mesh that is hollow at its box (a room, a tunnel, a house with an interior) is treated as solid: a probe inside the room near a wall would be pushed outside. `relocate` is therefore **off by default** and meant for scenes built from solid blocks (the city's lots, rocks, crates); indoors, classification and visibility already stop the leaks and the darkening, and the guide says to leave `relocate` off.

### 4. Following the camera: a toroidal volume in whole-cell steps (50g)

Options weighed:

| Option | Verdict |
|---|---|
| A. Move `bounds` with the camera and let the key refill | **No.** Every move refills all probes (1024 to 8192 probes at 32 a frame at best), and the volume does not light while it fills. |
| B. Several fixed volumes along the streets | **No.** One volume per scene (bank 5, M-VISUAL3), and a fixed tiling does not follow the player. |
| **C. Snap the box to whole cells around the camera; keep each probe in a slot `world index mod counts`; on a step, invalidate and capture only the slab that scrolled in** | **Chosen.** The standard clipmap trick; the probes the camera keeps are untouched, the work per step is one slab, and the shader needs one more lane (the slot offset). |

- **Snapping (`gi_follow_origin`, `gi_probes.h`).** The follow box has the authored size (`bounds`' extents) and spacing; its x and z are re-centred on the camera of the frame's **first view** (`out_.first`, the one that already owns the rolling update), snapped to whole cells **in f64 world coordinates** (`View3D::position` plus `Forward3D::origin_`), so a floating-origin rebase moves nothing. y stays authored. The key (`gi_key`) of a following volume hashes the spacing, counts, y band, version and bake, not the snapped origin: a step is a scroll, not a refill.
- **Slots and the scroll.** A probe with world cell index `i` lives in texel slot `i mod counts` per axis; `p_gi_min.w` carries `origin cell mod counts` packed (Design 2) and the shader adds it before the modulo. When the snapped origin moves by `k` cells on an axis (`k` capped at the count: a jump of a whole box is a refill), the `k` slabs that left are the slabs that arrive: one small pass per slab writes texel 9's r = 1 there (the slab of an x step is a column of `T` texels over every row, of a z step `counts[1]` contiguous rows), so the lookup switches those probes off at once; then the capture queue serves the scrolled-in probes (nearest the camera first) **before** the rolling update. `stats.gi_scrolled` counts the invalidated probes. Until a slab is captured, points near the box's leading edge take the sky's ambient (Design 2, "no probe left"), half a box away from the camera.
- **Budget, honestly.** Each slab of 32 x 8 x 32 probes is 256 probes; with visibility a probe costs about twice the uniform uploads of today's capture (the vis pass draws the same list). What the capture budget yields per frame in the city is not known: 50c measures it (`stats.gi_probes` in `09_openworld` on the fill and on rolling updates). If a car (30 m/s) crosses cells faster than slabs can be captured, the invalid band grows toward the camera and the GI degrades to the sky's ambient, never to wrong light. 50i picks the counts and the box from that measurement with the rule "a slab per crossing at 15 m/s (a fast run, a slow car) fits in the measured budget with a third left for rolling updates", and writes the numbers into its as-built entry; 32 x 8 x 32 over 192 x 28 m (6.2 m on x and z, 4 m on y, the floor 0.5 m above the street) is the plan's target, 32 x 4 x 32 the fallback.
- **Tiers and bakes.** `apply_quality` turns `follow` off where `updates` is off: the Low tier keeps the authored box at the entity, with its bake. The extract's off-screen reach (`gi_reach`) follows the snapped box (50h, through the same helper), so captures near the leading edge see the meshes there.

### 5. The kit: a separate `GiVisibility3D` component, not new `GiVolume3D` fields (50h)

- **Why separate.** `GiVolume3D` lives in `kit3d.h` (253 TUs) and is hashed and saved by its bytes (`sizeof` 88, pinned by `test_padding`): new fields would change the state hash and the saved bytes of every scene with a volume. A component in its own narrow header, read **on the `GiVolume3D`'s entity**, keeps every existing scene, hash and file as it is (the M-WATER2 `WaterFlow` rule).
- **Fields.** `GiVisibility3D` (`mce/kit/gi_visibility.h`): `enabled` 1 (u32), `relocate` 0 (u32), `max_offset` 0.4 (f32, Range 0..0.45, "fraction of the probe spacing a probe may move out of a solid"), `follow_camera` 0 (u32, "1: the volume's box follows the primary camera in whole cells, on x and z"), explicit zeroed padding and a `test_padding` entry. Present and enabled on the volume's entity: `GiVolume::visibility = true` (visibility and classification come together; no separate switch), `relocate`, `max_offset` and `follow` from the fields. Pure data, no system, no state. Registered next to `GiVolume3D` in `kit3d.cpp` (the registration list; `kit3d.h` untouched).
- **Feed.** `Kit3DExtract` (`.cpp` only) fills the carrier fields after mapping `GiVolume3D`; in bake mode `follow` is off (a bake is of the authored box); `apply_quality` follows. With `follow` the reach uses `gi_follow_origin` on the primary camera.

### Cvars, stats, tiers

- **Cvar** next to `cv_gi` in `forward3d.cpp` (no header): `r.gi.visibility` (bool, default 1, "Irradiance-volume visibility, probe classification and relocation for volumes that ask for them (GiVisibility3D); 0 = today's lookup"). At 0 Forward3D treats the volume as if `visibility` and `relocate` were false (the key changes, the volume refills once); `follow` stays (it needs the state texel only for the scroll, flag 1 without flag 2 makes the lookup skip just the invalid slab). Not `RequiresRestart`: the vis pipelines are created with the GI pipelines whenever `r.gi` is on.
- **Stats** (50b, `forward3d.h`): `Forward3DStats::gi_visibility` (bool: this frame's lookup used the visibility weight), `gi_relocated` (u32: probes moved by the latest fill or slab), `gi_scrolled` (u32: probes invalidated by a scroll this frame), `gi_vis_draws` (u32: draws of this frame's vis passes). They let the tests ask what ran without a pixel guess.
- **Tiers.** No `QualitySettings` row. Low: no captures, so no vis pass and no follow (`apply_quality`); the lookup uses the record when the bake carries it. Medium and High: everything the component asks for.
- **Defaults.** Everything is off unless a `GiVisibility3D` is on the volume's entity (or a test sets the carrier fields). The goldens stay byte-identical because no golden scene sets them; the new behaviour is pinned by measured GL tests and one new golden (`gi_thin_wall`), not by moving an old one.

### Determinism

Rendering is outside the state hash and the replay. The vis pass, the projection and the lookup use positions, the faces and constants (no time, no `rand`). Relocation is a pure f32 function of the draw list in draw order (ties by index); the follow snap is f64 world arithmetic (rebase-invariant). `GiVisibility3D` is saved data with no system: the extract reads it and writes nothing to the world, so a camera that walks the volume across the city leaves the state hash exactly as without the extract running. A scene without the component hashes, saves and renders exactly as today.

## Risks

- **The lit shader's length, shared.** 240 bytes of room today; any concurrent lit-shader change competes for it. 50d makes room first and says the numbers; a lit PR that merges in between re-measures after merging `master`. If the room is still short, the next lever is to splice the GI lookup as its own macro with its comments outside (the M-TRANSLUCENT recipe), not to cut features.
- **Capture throughput in a dense scene.** The budget (1024 uploads a frame, 96 draws a face) may give only a handful of probes a frame in the city, and visibility doubles a filled probe's cost. 50c measures before 50g and 50i commit to counts; the degraded mode (an uncaptured slab falls back to the sky's ambient half a box away) is safe but visible as a band of flatter light at speed. Cheaper captures (sharing the object uniforms between the lit and the vis pass) are follow-up 7.
- **Lookup cost.** Today 32 fetches a pixel; with the record 72 (80 with offsets), plus the Chebyshev math, for every pixel inside a visibility volume. M-VISUAL measured the GI lookup at +69 ms of llvmpipe's main pass on `04_cubes`; real-GPU cost is an estimate (0.2 to 0.5 ms at 1080p on a mid GPU), **measured on the GPU list**. The flag keeps every other volume at today's cost.
- **A coarse record.** Six lobes from 16 x 16 faces cannot resolve a narrow gap or a doorway; Chebyshev can over-darken corners (light bleeding the other way). The constants (lobe power, `dmax`, minimum variance, crush) are tuned in 50e against the thin-wall, buried and open tests; if a doorway case reads wrong, follow-up 2 (octahedral depth) is the real answer.
- **Hollow meshes and relocation.** The OBB rule treats a room as solid. Opt-in, documented, off by default; classification still covers indoor scenes.
- **Moving occluders excluded.** A door that closes after the fill does not occlude in the record until a refill (`version`), and a parked bus is not an occluder at all. Recorded; follow-up 6.
- **Stale bakes.** A volume baked before it asked for visibility has the wrong width: refused with the warning, and on Low the scene draws without GI until re-baked. The guide says to re-bake after adding the component.
- **Translation.** Integer flags and modulo in the lookup, a `CullMode::None` vis pipeline and `gl_FrontFacing` in a new fragment shader on Metal and WebGPU: GPU list.
