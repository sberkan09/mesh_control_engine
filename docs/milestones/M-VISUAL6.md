# M-VISUAL6: open-world glass, lawn and kerb read (milestone record)

Added 2026-11-28, owner: the M-VISUAL routine 2 thread, from the candidates listed at the end of [M-VISUAL5.md](M-VISUAL5.md). The judgement rests on the day frame `notes/audit-day-2.png` (150 frames of `scenes/city.mcscene` on llvmpipe); no side-by-side with another game was made. Each step is measured on a fresh frame before and after, and a step that leaves the frame unchanged is recorded as tried, not shipped.

| # | Step | State |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ground-floor shopfront glass is a flat dark navy with no room behind it. Try interior mapping (`mce_interior_cells`) on the ground-floor panes in `gen_buildings.py`, keeping the dark read | analysed 2026-11-28, not shipped: the shop texture is one 12 m x 6 m tile (4 bays across, one row), and `interior_cells` makes square cells in uv, so 4 cells give 3 m x 1.5 m rooms and a 2.7 m pane (sill 0.7 m) would show two stacked rooms; 2 cells put the pane across the 3 m row boundary. A shop pane needs a non-square or offset cell grid in the interior shader (render core) or a square 12 m shop tile with the pane inside one 3 m row. In the day frame the dark glass also reads plausibly as shopfront glass, so this is low priority |
| 2 | Lawn macro variation: patches or mowing bands. `Decal3D` patches did not show (M-VISUAL5 step 2) | done 2026-11-28 (#1381): no shader change was needed, 112 thin `Lawn band` slabs (5 m wide, alternating) carry a per-instance `MeshRef.tint` so the lawn shows mowing stripes (`notes/lawnbands-after.png`) |
| 3 | Kerb and asphalt wear: lighter marks (chips, salt, gum) or a shader edge term; dark grime had no contrast on the dark asphalt (M-VISUAL5 step 5) | tried 2026-11-28, not shipped: a pale kerb-chips `Decal3D` (new texture `kerb_chips`, 8 per kerb side per road, drawn after the older decals) changed 11 pixels with fleck-sized marks and 107 with a pale band plus 4-texel chips along the kerb (`notes/kerbchips-after.png`), neither visible in the start frame. A diagnostic with the same decals as 3 m bright slabs at 0.3 of the half-width shows them (so decals render and the cap is not the cause); the marks at the kerb edge are lost beside the bright kerb bevel and the very dark asphalt. Next try: a shader-side edge term on the road material (render core) |
