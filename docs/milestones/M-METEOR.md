# M-METEOR — An opt-in meteor shower: shooting stars across the night sky, drawn as a view-direction layer (milestone record)

**Status:** planned (2026-10-30), steps 18a to 18e. **Owner: routine 2 thread (the M-VISUAL line).** Follow-up of M-NIGHTSKY (the moon, the star field and the night fade the gate reads), M-AURORA and M-RAINBOW (the sky-layer pattern: kit component, `render::` carrier seam, `Kit3DExtract` feed, one shader step, Luau) and M-WEATHER (the rain and clouds the level reads). Today the night sky has the gradient, the moon and fixed twinkling stars (M-NIGHTSKY) and the aurora (M-AURORA); nothing in it ever moves quickly, and `grep -ri meteor src tests samples` finds nothing. No Linear issues (free issue limit); this record is the tracker. The shader step is Opus-written and merged after an independent Opus review plus a green `--task` (as M-CLOUDS 9b, M-NIGHTSKY 14d, M-WATER 15c, M-AURORA 16c and M-RAINBOW 17c).

**Goal.** An opt-in kit component `Meteor` describes shooting stars: how many per minute, how bright, how long the streak is and how fast it flies. Pure, bit-stable helpers turn a time slot and a slot index into a meteor (start direction, travel direction, start time and duration) with an integer hash, and turn the night fade, cloud cover and rain into a level. `Kit3DExtract` hands the result to the renderer as a small `render::MeteorSky` carrier and one shader step draws a few simultaneous streaks per pixel as a distance-to-segment glow with a fading tail, additive in the sky pass in the shared `MCE_GLSL_SKY_LAYERS` string, so the water reflects them with no second copy. Deterministic, saved with the scene, opt-in; a scene without `Meteor` hashes, simulates and renders exactly as today.

**Not in scope:** meteor showers with a radiant or a calendar (the rate is an authored number; every streak starts anywhere in the up-going sky), fireballs, smoke trains, sound, meteors lighting the ground, clouds or the water (the layer is sky emission only; the water gets the reflection through the sky function), meteors reaching the ground or impacts, parallax (the streak is a pure function of the view direction and the time, like the stars and the aurora), meteors in the IBL bake, reflection probes or GI captures (main sky pass and water reflection only, like clouds, the night sky, the aurora and the rainbow), meteors under an equirect `sky_texture` (the texture wins), more than a fixed few simultaneous streaks (see Design), per-meteor authoring or a gameplay hook ("wish on a falling star" events), and changing `Weather`, `WetSurfaces`, `WorldClock`, `DayCycle`, `Moon`, `Aurora`, `Rainbow`, `Water` or `Environment3D` bytes (the last is a wide header).

## Design

- **Opt-in component, no system, no state.** New component `Meteor` (`mce/kit/meteor.h`, one per scene, the first in ECS order is read, registered by `install_weather` next to `Aurora` and `Rainbow`; 18a confirms the spot by reading `install_weather`). Authoring (all `Save`, with a `Range`): `enabled` 1; `rate` 4 (0..30, meteors per minute across the whole visible sky; 0 draws none); `brightness` 1 (0..4, gain); `length` 1 (0.5..2, scales the angular length of the head-to-tail streak); `speed` 1 (0.5..2, scales the angular speed, so a faster meteor lasts a shorter time); `min_darkness` 0.6 (0..1, the night fade below which no meteor shows; a smoothstep from this value to 1 so they appear as the sky darkens enough for stars); `night_only` 1 (0..1, 1 = scaled by the night gate; 0 lets a scene force it for a screenshot, as for `Aurora`); `seed` 0 (0..65535, folded into the hash so two scenes differ). No engine-written fields; the struct stays naturally packed with an explicit zeroed pad and a `test_padding` entry.
- **Night facts the gate reads.** The sky has no latitude or sky-darkness resource. The night fade is the one `Kit3DExtract` already computes for the night sky and the aurora: `1 - smoothstep(-0.1, 0.1, sun_elevation)` with `sun_elevation = -render::sun_direction_at(time_of_day).y`, and 0 when there is no `WorldClock` (`time_of_day` -1; the guide says to add a clock or set `night_only` 0, as for the aurora). A `Moon` is not needed. Moonlight is not read: a bright full moon washes out faint streaks in the real sky, and this is left to the look (a follow-up may dim by `moon_level`); this is recorded rather than special-cased. The extract's cloud coverage and `Weather` rain intensity are the other two inputs.
- **Pure helpers (kit, 18a).** In `meteor.h`, inline, f32 (the hash is integer), fixed evaluation order, no `rand`, no `sin()` of an unbounded argument, no hash of pointers:
  - `meteor_level(const Meteor&, f32 night_fade, f32 cloud_coverage, f32 rain_intensity) -> f32` is `enabled * night_gate * (1 - cloud_coverage)^2 * (1 - rain_intensity)` where `night_gate = smoothstep(min_darkness, 1, night_fade)` (1 when `night_only` is 0). Inputs are clamped to 0..1 (NaN counts as 0), a zero `rate`, `brightness` or `enabled` 0 returns exactly 0, which the render off-check relies on. Brightness and the rate are not folded into the level: the shader reads them from the carrier.
  - `meteor_wrap_time(f64 t) -> f32`: the time wrapped in f64 to `kMeteorPeriodSeconds` (60 s, the same period `star_time` uses, so the slot grid below divides it exactly); NaN and infinity count as 0.
  - `meteor_slot(f32 t_wrapped) -> {slot, frac}`: slots are `kMeteorSlotSeconds` (5 s) long, 12 per period, so the slot number wraps with the period and the pattern repeats exactly after it.
  - `meteor_slot_count(const Meteor&) -> u32` how many of the `kMeteorMaxPerSlot` (3, equal to the shader's simultaneous streak count) candidate indices are active in a slot: `rate` meteors per minute is `rate / 12` per 5 s slot, so index `k` is active when `k < floor(rate/12)` or, for the fractional part, when the slot's hash draw is below it (so `rate` 4 is one meteor in a third of the slots on average; the maximum `rate` 30 gives 2.5 per slot, within 3).
  - `meteor_make(const Meteor&, u32 slot, u32 index) -> MeteorStreak`: a pcg2d hash (the `aurora_hash` family, mirrored in the shader, one integer function) of `(slot, index, seed)` gives, in a fixed order of draws, the start direction (azimuth uniform, elevation from `asin` of a uniform in [0.1, 0.95] so streaks start away from the horizon), the travel direction (a unit tangent at the start, from a uniform angle), the start time within the slot (0..0.5 of the slot, 2.5 s, so with the longest duration of 2.4 s (1.2 s at `speed` 0.5) a streak ends inside its own slot), the duration (0.4..1.2 s divided by `speed`), and an `active` flag. The streak is a great-circle arc: the head position at slot fraction `s` is the start rotated about `cross(start, travel)` by `angular_speed * (s - start_time)`; the helper returns head position, tail position (`length * 8` degrees behind the head along the arc) and a brightness envelope (fast rise, slow fall). Great-circle motion needs only `sin` / `cos` of an angle bounded by 45 degrees, so no large argument exists.
  - `meteor_head(const MeteorStreak&, f32 age) -> vec3` and `meteor_envelope(const MeteorStreak&, f32 age) -> f32`, 0 outside `[0, duration]`.
  A zero `rate`, a zero `brightness` and `enabled` 0 make every `active` flag false. NaN, inverted and zero ranges fall back to the defaults.
- **Render carrier (seam, 18b).** `render::MeteorSky {level, rate, brightness, length, speed, time, seed, enabled, pad_}` in `scene3d.h` next to `AuroraSky` and `RainbowSky`, `set_meteor()` / `meteor()`, `QualitySettings::meteor` and cvar `r.meteor` (Low is off; `apply_quality` by-value overload clamps ranges, NaN to 0, and zeroes `level` for `r.meteor 0` or the Low tier), exactly the shape of 16b and 17b. `scene3d.h` is a wide header (`include_fanout.py` reported 147 of 900 TUs at M-RAINBOW 17b, under the 150 warning but a rebuild of every consumer, and close to it), so this ships as its own small seam PR merged first, with the reason and the current number in the description; if the number has reached 150 the PR says so and still goes first, and the milestone does not add more `scene3d.h` fields after it. `level` 0 or `enabled` false means off: the frame is the one without it.
- **Extract feed (kit, 18b).** `Kit3DExtract::apply_environment` fills `render::MeteorSky` from the first `Meteor`, the night fade it already computes for the night sky and the aurora, the cloud coverage it already computes (after `apply_quality`) and the first `Weather`'s rain intensity (`.cpp` only). `time` is `meteor_wrap_time` of the value `set_time` gets later in the same extract (as the aurora's and the star time, because `Scene3D::time()` still holds last frame's value), so it is the existing frame time, not a new clock, and it does not depend on a `Moon` (the night sky's `star_time` is only set with a `Moon`). Nothing is set with no `Meteor`, `enabled` 0, a level of 0 or an equirect `sky_texture`; the carrier is written every frame (default when off) so a stale one never survives, as in 16b and 17b.
- **Sky shader (Opus, 18c).** `meteor_layer(vec3 c, vec3 d, float pix)` in the shared `MCE_GLSL_SKY_LAYERS` string behind `p_meteor.x > 0.0` so a frame without meteors is bit-identical. For an up-going ray `d` (`d.y > 0`) the layer evaluates the current slot's candidate streaks, a fixed `3` (`kMeteorMaxPerSlot`), only (a streak starts in the first half of its slot and lasts at most 2.4 s, so it always ends inside its slot and no previous-slot lookup is needed; 18c re-checks the constants). Per slot, per index: the integer hash (pcg2d, mirrored from the kit as one commented block with a pixel test that compares them at probe points, as 15c does for the wave table and 17c for the rainbow constants), the great-circle head position at the slot fraction, and the angular distance from `d` to the segment between tail and head (distance to a line segment on the sphere, in the tangent plane at the head, so it needs only dot and cross products). The glow is `exp2(-(dist / width)^2)` with `width = max(0.15 degrees, pix)`, so a streak never thins below a pixel and does not alias or flicker, times a tail fade along the segment (bright at the head, linear to 0 at the tail), times the envelope and `p_meteor.y` brightness. The layer is additive, scaled by `p_meteor.x` (level, which already folds the night gate, cloud cover and rain), fades toward the horizon with the same haze the stars use (`smoothstep(0, 0.25, d.y)`), and is applied in `kSkyFS` after the stars and the aurora and before `apply_clouds` and the fog, so a cloud in front dims it and fog hides it. `water_sky` calls the same function on the mirrored ray behind the same off-check. **PassU:** two new `vec4` (`p_meteor`: level, brightness, length, speed; `p_meteor2`: rate, wrapped time, seed, spare) taken from the 10 free `vec4` of the 1536-byte block (`p_free[10]` becomes `p_free[8]`, the size stays 1536); 18c re-checks the free count before writing the shader, updates `MCE_GLSL_PASS_U`, `Forward3DPassU`, the offset chain and both `static_assert`s together, and records the new free count. The time component is the existing frame time through the carrier, not a new PassU time slot. The Low tier draws none.
- **Luau (18d).** `weather.set_meteor{rate=, brightness=, length=, speed=, min_darkness=, night_only=, seed=, enabled=}`, getters `weather.meteor_<key>()` and read-only `weather.meteor_level()` from the same helper (the level reads the night fade of the current clock, the cloud coverage and the rain, and is 0 with no clock unless `night_only` is 0), named errors, values clamped to the Range, nothing written on error, a missing `Meteor` raises (the setter never adds one), typed in `definitions.cpp`, same rules as `weather.set_aurora` and `weather.set_rainbow`. The sample: `samples/12_weather` already carries a `Moon`, an `Aurora` and a `Rainbow` on the Weather entity and a clock; 18d adds a `Meteor` to it and a log line `meteor:` every 150 ticks (the level through the dawn and the rain), if its clock still passes a dark hour, otherwise a night capture of `samples/13_water` (the sea reflects the streaks) carries it. 18d decides and records, and only the chosen sample's expectations move.
- **Determinism and hash.** A scene without `Meteor` is byte-identical (one empty query in the extract; the extract is not part of the state hash). A scene with it differs only in the saved component and in frames. A streak is a pure function of authoring, the wrapped time and the integer hash of `(slot, index, seed)`, so two runs agree, a save/load gives the same values, and a test evaluates the helpers over a run in one go and in two halves. The time is wrapped in f64 on the CPU to 60 s, the slot grid divides the period, so the pattern repeats exactly after it and there is no float drift over long runs.

## Gates
- A scene without `Meteor` (or with `enabled` 0, a closed level, day, rain, thick cloud, `rate` 0) has an unchanged state hash and unchanged frames; every existing sample hash and golden stays byte-identical except `12_weather`'s expectations (18a scene, 18d log), and existing goldens under `tests/render_gl/*` stay byte-identical through 18b and 18c on both backends.
- `Weather`, `WetSurfaces`, `WorldClock`, `DayCycle`, `Moon`, `Aurora`, `Rainbow`, `CloudLayer`, `Water` and `Environment3D` are never written by meteor code (a test hashes them with and without `Meteor`).
- Level and every streak are pure functions of authoring, the night fade, cloud coverage, rain and the wrapped time; the hash is integer, no stored float, no wall clock, no `rand`. The shader and the kit helper agree (pixel test of 18c).
- PassU stays 1536 bytes with 8 free `vec4` afterwards; no other pass's block changes.
- Include fan-out: the only wide header touched is `scene3d.h`, in the 18b seam PR (checked with `scripts/ci/include_fanout.py`); `meteor.h` is included only by the extract, the install, the script binding and the tests.
- Slow tests: a GPU test over 60 s ships a smoke variant (one frame, one scene) and the long-time sweep is labelled `slow` with `mce_mark_slow`; the sample's added `meteor:` run does not lengthen its headless test.
- Luau: setters clamp, bad types raise named errors and write nothing.

## Risks
- **Main shader risk: the look and the cost.** The layer runs over every up-going sky pixel: 3 current-slot streaks, each a hash, a great-circle rotation and a segment distance. The per-streak head and tail are the same for every pixel of a frame, so 18c computes them once per fragment from uniform inputs only (the compiler hoists nothing across a loop, so the plan is a fixed unrolled loop with the streak maths kept to dot and cross products); 18c measures what it can and records that `perf_run` at 1080p needs a GPU box. Early out per pixel when the pixel is farther than the streak's bounding angle from the head, before the exact segment distance.
- **Thin fast lines alias and flicker.** A streak a fraction of a degree wide, moving fast, minified below a pixel crawls and pops between frames. 18c floors the width at the pixel footprint (`pix`), lets the brightness carry the energy lost to widening (so the streak stays visible), and includes the yaw frame-difference test and a low-render-scale check; the exposure of a fast streak across a frame is not simulated (no motion blur): the streak is a snapshot at the frame time, which the guide says.
- **Uniform vs hashed placement.** Uniform start directions on the sphere put most streaks near the zenith in screen space for a wide view and few near the horizon; the start elevation is drawn from an authored range (0.1..0.95 of the sine) for a look, not a physical radiant. The guide says so, and a follow-up may add a radiant.
- **Rate is an average.** The slot grid makes the count per 5 s a hash draw, so a quiet minute is possible; the test bounds the average over a long run, not each minute. A `rate` of 30 is the cap because 3 streaks per slot is the shader's fixed count.
- **Tail across the slot boundary.** A streak must end inside its slot or the next slot's lookup would have to draw it. The start window (first 0.5 of the 5 s slot, 2.5 s) plus the longest duration (1.2 s divided by the smallest `speed` 0.5, 2.4 s) is 4.9 s, under the slot; 18a fixes the constants and tests start offset plus duration is at most one slot over the full hash range and every `speed`, and the shader then needs only the current slot.
- **Sun and night gate.** With no clock there is no night, so no meteors by default; the guide and the Luau help text say to add a clock or set `night_only` 0. A moon at full washes streaks out in life; moonlight is not read here (recorded, a follow-up).
- **Interaction with the stars, aurora, clouds and fog.** The streaks must sit after the stars and aurora (additive over them) and before the cloud composite and the fog; a mis-ordered line would show them through a deck or through fog. Tests cover clouds and fog and the ordering is part of the Opus review.
- **`scene3d.h` is near the warning.** 147 of 900 TUs at 17b; the seam adds one small struct. If the number crosses 150 the walls stage warns; the seam PR is the only one allowed to touch it.
