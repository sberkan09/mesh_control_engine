# M-FREEZE — Water that freezes in winter: calm, pale, rough ice from the season, with no shader (milestone record)

**Status:** planned (2026-10-30), steps 20a to 20d. **Owner: routine 2 thread (the M-VISUAL line).** Follow-up of M-WATER (`Water`, `render::WaterSurface`, the extract feed), M-SEASONS (`Season`, `season_phase`, the winter weight), M-SNOWCOVER and M-WEATHER (the cold look it matches), M-GROUNDMIST (the pure-helper, no-shader, no-system pattern). Today the sea of `13_water` looks the same in July and in January: M-SEASONS tints foliage and frosts the ground in winter, but the water keeps its waves, its blue and its shore foam. No Linear issues (free issue limit); this record is the tracker.

**Goal.** An opt-in kit component `WaterIce` turns the season (the `Season` winter weight at the `WorldClock` day and hour) into a freeze amount 0..1, with a manual floor for a scene that wants a permanently frozen lake. Pure helpers turn the amount into an ice look: wave amplitude, steepness and speed scaled toward 0, the shallow and deep colours mixed toward a pale ice colour, the depth scale shortened so the tint is opaque, the roughness raised, the sky reflection lowered and the shore foam faded. `Kit3DExtract` applies the look to the *local `render::WaterSurface` only*, before `render::apply_quality`. No new shader, pass, render field or system state. A scene without `WaterIce` hashes, simulates and renders exactly as today.

**Not in scope:** a shader or a new `render::` field or struct change (an ice texture, cracks, a frost normal map, a refraction change or a mesh swap all need one; the look is a recolouring and a calming of the existing water), partial freezing from the shore inwards or per-region freezing (one global amount; the shore line comes from the terrain depth test and is not moved), snow lying on the ice (a follow-up could reuse the snow cover), breaking ice and walkable-ice physics (`water.height_at`, `water.submerged` and buoyancy keep reading the authored waves, see Risks), temperature or a freeze threshold read from `Weather` (the `Season` and the manual floor are the only drivers), steam or breath particles, sounds, and changing `Water`, `Season`, `Weather`, `SnowCover`, `WorldClock`, `Environment3D` or `Scene3D` bytes. `kit3d.h`, `scene3d.h`, `weather.h`, `water.h` and `season.h` are not edited.

## Design

- **Real names read while planning.** `render::WaterSurface` (`mce/render/scene3d.h`) is already a per-frame carrier with `amplitude`, `steepness`, `speed_scale`, `shallow_color`, `deep_color`, `depth_scale_m`, `reflectivity`, `roughness`, `foam_amount`, `foam_width_m`, `time` and `enabled`. The extract (`kit3d_extract.cpp`, the block commented "water (M-WATER 15b)") reads the first `Water`, fills a local `render::WaterSurface surf` from it and calls `scene.set_water(render::apply_quality(quality_, surf))`; the `Water` component is never written. Every field the ice look needs is therefore already on the carrier: **frozen water needs no shader and no render-struct change**; it is a set of multiplications and mixes on the local `surf` between the field copies and `apply_quality`, the same kind of edit as `apply_wind_sway` on a batch. `Season` (`mce/kit/season.h`) has `season_phase(const Season&, u32 day, f32 hour)` and the internal winter weight `detail::season_winter_weight(phase)` (0 up to phase 0.5, 1 at the centre of winter 0.875, 0 at the year end); `WorldClock` (`mce/kit/world_clock.h`) has `u32 day` and `f32 hour`. `install_weather` (`weather.cpp`) registers the weather-line components next to `Water` and `Season`.
- **Opt-in component, no system, no state.** New component `WaterIce` (`mce/kit/water_ice.h`, one per scene, the first in ECS order is read, registered by `install_weather` next to `Water` and `Season`; 20a confirms the spot by reading `install_weather`). Pure data: the freeze amount is a pure function of authoring and the clock, evaluated at extract and in the Luau getter, so nothing is carried between ticks and nothing is hashed beyond the authored fields. Authoring (all `Save`, with a `Range`): `enabled` 1; `freeze_start` 0.5 and `freeze_full` 0.9 (0..1, the winter weight at which ice starts to form and at which the water is fully frozen; `freeze_full` below `freeze_start` is swapped); `manual` 0 (0..1, a floor for the amount, so a still scene or a script can freeze the water with no `Season`); `ice_color` (default 0.62 0.78 0.85, linear), `ice_roughness` 0.35 (0..1), `ice_reflectivity` 0.55 (0..1, the reflection of fully frozen water as a share of the authored one), `ice_depth_m` 0.4 (0.05..10, the depth scale of fully frozen water, short so the tint is opaque and the sea floor is not seen), `calm` 1 (0..1, how far the waves are flattened at full freeze; 1 = flat), `strength` 1 (0..1, scales every effect; 0 = off exactly). No engine-written fields; naturally packed with an explicit zeroed pad and a `test_padding` entry.
- **Freeze amount (kit, 20a).** `water_ice_amount(const WaterIce&, const Season*, f32 phase) -> f32` is `max(manual, smoothstep(freeze_start, freeze_full, winter_weight(phase)))` times `strength`. The winter weight is read from `season.h` (`detail::season_winter_weight`, reachable because `season.h` is a plain header; it is not edited). With no `Season` the amount is the manual floor. NaN, inverted and zero ranges fall back to the defaults; the smoothstep is a polynomial (no `pow`, `exp` or trig, no `rand`), fixed evaluation order. `enabled` 0, `strength` 0 and a missing component return exactly 0.
- **Ice look (kit, 20a).** `water_ice_look(const WaterIce&, f32 amount) -> WaterIceLook {amplitude_scale, steepness_scale, speed_scale, ice_mix, roughness_floor, reflectivity_scale, depth_scale_m, foam_scale}` with `a` the amount: `amplitude_scale = steepness_scale = speed_scale = 1 - a * calm`; `ice_mix = a` (the mix factor of both colours toward `ice_color`); `roughness_floor = ice_roughness * a` (the extract takes `max(authored, floor)`); `reflectivity_scale = 1 - a * (1 - ice_reflectivity)`; `depth_scale_m` is the authored depth scale lerped toward `ice_depth_m` by `a` (the extract does the lerp, the helper returns `a` and the target); `foam_scale = 1 - a` (the shore foam fades first as ice forms). `a == 0` gives the identity as exact values (1, 1, 1, 0, 0, 1, authored, 1), which the bit-identical gates rely on.
- **Extract (kit, 20b).** Inside the existing water block, after the field copies and before `render::apply_quality`, the extract reads the first `WaterIce`, the first `Season` and the first `WorldClock` once per frame (`.cpp` only), computes the phase from the clock (day 0 hour 0 without a clock, as M-SEASONS does), the amount and the look, and edits the local `surf`: `amplitude`, `steepness` and `speed_scale` multiplied; `shallow_color` and `deep_color` mixed toward `ice_color` by `ice_mix`; `depth_scale_m` lerped; `roughness = max(roughness, roughness_floor)`; `reflectivity` multiplied; `foam_amount` multiplied. `surf.time`, `wave_count`, `wavelength`, `wind_dir`, `level_y` and `half_extent` are untouched. With no `Water` nothing is drawn, as today, and the `WaterIce` is ignored; with no `WaterIce`, `enabled` 0, `strength` 0 or an amount of 0 the code path is skipped entirely, so frames are bit-identical. `Water`, `Season`, `WorldClock` and `Weather` are never written.
- **Why no system.** The day count and hour are exact, so the amount is deterministic by itself; a freeze system would only add state to hash and a way to drift, and the ice would not follow a script that sets the clock. The `Water`, `Season` and `WorldClock` column hashes are unchanged by construction.
- **Determinism and hash.** A scene without `WaterIce` is byte-identical (one empty query in the extract; the extract is not part of the state hash). A scene with it differs only in the saved component and in frames. The amount depends only on authoring, `day` and `hour`, so two runs agree and save/load mid-winter gives the same amount; a test evaluates the helpers over a `WorldClock` run in one go and in two halves.
- **Quality tiers.** Recolouring costs nothing, so every tier keeps it. The look is applied before `apply_quality`, so the Low tier's and `r.water`'s existing handling applies to the already-frozen carrier unchanged.
- **Luau (20c).** `weather.set_water_ice{enabled=, freeze_start=, freeze_full=, manual=, ice_color={r,g,b}, ice_roughness=, ice_reflectivity=, ice_depth_m=, calm=, strength=}`, getters `weather.water_ice_<key>()` (the colour returns `r, g, b`) and read-only `weather.water_frozen()` (the current amount 0..1 from the same helper on the current `WorldClock` day and hour, so a script can stop a boat or switch an ice-skating rule; the manual floor with no clock or no `Season`, nil-free), named errors, values clamped to the Range, nothing written on error, a missing `WaterIce` raises (the setter never adds one), typed in the script definitions next to `weather.set_season`, same rules as `weather.set_season` and `weather.set_mist`. `samples/13_water` gets a `WaterIce`, a `Season` and a `WorldClock` (short `year_days`, so the sea freezes and thaws in a minute or two) and logs `ice:` (the amount) every 150 ticks; its golden and log expectations move in 20c, nothing else. 20c reads the sample first: `13_water` has a fixed sun and no clock today, so adding the clock must not move the sun (no `DayCycle`, and `Environment3D.time_of_day` stays -1); if it would, the sample sets `manual` instead of a clock and the Season is left out.

## Gates
- A scene without `WaterIce` (or with `enabled` 0, `strength` 0, or an amount of 0) has an unchanged state hash and unchanged frames; every existing sample hash and golden stays byte-identical until 20a / 20c change `samples/13_water`, and only that sample's expectations move.
- `Water`, `Season`, `Weather`, `SnowCover`, `WorldClock` and `Environment3D` are never written by ice code: the extract changes only the local `render::WaterSurface`; a test hashes the components and resources with and without `WaterIce`.
- The amount and every helper are pure functions of authoring, `day` and `hour`; no stored float, no wall clock, no `rand`.
- No shader, pass, `PassU` change or new render field is added; the only render-side values are existing `render::WaterSurface` members.
- `kit3d.h`, `scene3d.h`, `weather.h`, `water.h` and `season.h` are not edited (checked with `scripts/ci/include_fanout.py`); `water_ice.h` is included only by the extract, the install, the script binding and the tests, so no seam PR is expected.
- Luau: setters clamp, bad types raise named errors and write nothing.

## Risks
- **The plane is still a plane.** Ice here is a calm, opaque, pale, rough water surface: no cracks, no frost texture, no refraction. Under a low sun the sky reflection of the flattened surface can still read as a mirror; `ice_roughness` and `ice_reflectivity` are tuned against the `13_water` sky in 20c. A real ice material is a render milestone.
- **Gameplay still sees waves.** `water.height_at`, `water.normal_at` and `water.submerged` read the authored `Water`, so a crate that bobs on the sea keeps bobbing on frozen water. The milestone does not change them (it would put the freeze into gameplay results); `weather.water_frozen()` lets a script stop the bobbing or change the rules, and the guide says so. A hashed or gameplay-visible freeze is a recorded follow-up.
- **Sea floor and shore.** The shortened `ice_depth_m` hides the depth tint, but the terrain depth test still cuts the plane at the shore line, so a freezing shore does not advance inland. Shore foam fades with the amount, which is the only shore cue.
- **Needs a driver.** With no `Season` and `manual` 0 nothing freezes; with no `WorldClock` the season is fixed at `start_phase`. With the default 1200 s day a 12-day year is 4 real hours, so the sample shortens `year_days` or the day length.
- **Colour fights `DayCycle`.** `DayCycle` rewrites sun, ambient and fog colours only, not water colours, so there is no writer to fight; the pale ice colour under a warm dusk key is tuned in 20c.
- **Quality tiers.** The look is applied before `apply_quality`; if `apply_quality` ever replaces the water fields on a tier, the order is rechecked in 20b.
