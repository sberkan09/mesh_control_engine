# M-KILLCOUNT: kill counter on the 09_openworld HUD (2026-10-31)

`Wanted3D.kills` (engine-written, saved, ReadOnly) counts the deaths the entity caused: every `Died3D` whose `killer` resolves to the entity holding `Wanted3D` (its shots, its rams), whatever team the victim is on, never itself. It counts whether or not police saw it; heat rules are unchanged. Wanted3D grows from 68 to 72 bytes (no padding).

Sample: the HUD has a "KILLS n" text at the bottom left bound to `Wanted3D/kills` (generated by `gen_assets.py`).

Test: `tests/kit/test_wanted3d.cpp` ("Wanted3D.kills counts the deaths the entity caused").

Limits: not split by team; deaths by fire or a car the player used after leaving it are not credited (whatever `Died3D.killer` says).
