# M-SHOTMARKS: bullet holes where shots land

**Status:** done (2026-11-10), tracer line left open. Closes the "no tracer or bullet-hole decal" limit left by [M-SHOTFX](M-SHOTFX.md) and
[M-IMPACTFX](M-IMPACTFX.md) (sparks exist, nothing stays on the wall). Game layer only: one new kit module and the
`samples/09_openworld` scene. No ECS, render, physics or audio core; the render side already draws `Decal3D`. Needs no device or
account. Sits beside, and does not touch, the M-VISUAL work (lighting, reflections): it only places existing decals.

## Why

A shot against a wall, car or the ground leaves a spark for a moment and then nothing. `WeaponHit3D` carries the point and the
normal, so a pooled decal can be placed there and stay for a while.

## Design

`BulletHole3D` on each of N authored entities that also carry a `Decal3D` and a `LocalTransform` (the ring: no owner entity and no
entity references to save). Fields: `size`, `depth`, `life`, `fade`, `alpha`, and engine-written `age` and `serial` (28 bytes). System
`kit_bullet_holes` (FixedUpdate_Post) reads `WeaponHit3D`, takes the slot with the lowest `serial` (never used first, then the oldest),
moves it to the hit point, turns its +Y along the normal (decals project down local -Y), sizes and shows its decal and stamps
`serial` as the highest plus one, so a save and resume keeps the order without a stored cursor. Hits whose target has `Health3D` leave
no hole (blood is another milestone).

## Decisions taken (change them in the plan PR if wrong)

- Decals, not geometry: `Decal3D` already projects onto whatever is below it, so holes follow curved and moving surfaces for free; a hole on a
  moving car does not follow the car (acceptable, it fades).
- A ring of fixed size, not spawn and destroy: no allocation in play and a bounded draw cost.
- Hits on characters leave nothing, a later blood milestone can reuse the same ring.
- The look is a first guess; it is judged from a capture in the cloud, not on a real display.
