# M-CROSSHAIR: aim point on the 09_openworld HUD (2026-10-31)

`hud.mcui` (generated by `gen_assets.py`) gets a white "+" at the middle of the screen. Shots leave the camera along its forward axis (`Weapon3D.aim_from`), so the middle of the screen is where they land. Data only; covered by the existing sample tests.

Limits: always visible (also on foot without the gun in hand and in a car); no spread bloom, no hit marker (`WeaponHit3D` is not a UI signal yet).
