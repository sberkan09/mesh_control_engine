# M-HEATHAZE — Heat haze: the air above a fire, an engine or hot sand bends the picture behind it (milestone record)

**Status:** 43a and 43b done (2026-11-06; data seam, pure helpers and quality tiers), 43c done (2026-11-06; the pass, awaiting its independent Opus review), 43d to 43f planned. **Owner: routine 2 thread (the M-VISUAL line).** Follow-up of M-LENSRAIN (the same post-pass pattern), M-LENSFLARE (the prepass depth read) and the 3D visual assessment (`assessments/3d-gorsellik-degerlendirmesi.md`, item 8). Today the renderer has fire and smoke particles, emissive glow and bloom, but a fire, a car exhaust or a desert road never bends the air: nothing in the frame refracts what lies behind a hot spot. No Linear issues (free issue limit); this record is the tracker.

**Naming:** no type, file or milestone called `HeatHaze`, `heat_haze` or `HazeVolume` exists (`src/`, `docs/`, `tests/`, `samples/` checked 2026-11-06; the only hits are prose about shimmer in the M-TRANSLUCENT and M-OUTLINE records). The render setting is `render::HeatHaze` (in `PostFx::haze`, an array of volumes), the pure helpers live in `mce/render/heat_haze.h`, the kit component is `HeatHaze3D` (`mce/kit/heat_haze.h`).

**Goal.** An opt-in screen-space refraction pass. Each haze volume is a world-space sphere (centre, radius, strength, rise speed). Behind a volume the picture is displaced by a smooth, slowly rising noise field, so a campfire wobbles what is seen through the air above it and a hot road ripples the horizon. The displacement is masked by depth: only pixels whose scene depth is farther than the volume's near surface are displaced, so an object standing in front of the haze stays sharp and the haze does not tear the silhouette of what is in front. The pass runs on the HDR frame **before bloom** (after the flare and the lens rain), so bloom and the tonemapper act on the bent picture. **Render-side only: nothing is written to a component and nothing is hashed.** With `PostFx::haze.count` 0 (the default), no `HeatHaze3D`, the Low tier or `r.heat_haze 0`, the pass is not recorded and the frame is bit-identical to today.

**Not in scope:** haze that bends the shadow maps or the reflections (screen space only), refraction of particles drawn after the pass (the transparent pass is already in the HDR frame when the pass runs, so particles behind a volume do bend; particles in front of it are masked by the same depth test only where they write depth, which transparent ones do not: recorded as a limit), a per-material haze, box or capsule volumes (spheres only), volumes that follow a physics body automatically (the kit reads the entity's transform each frame, so a parent does move it), and a Luau API beyond the component fields.

## Design

- **Render setting (seam, 43a).** A POD `render::HeatHaze` in `scene3d.h` just above `PostFx`, and `HeatHaze haze;` in `PostFx` after `rain`. `u32 count` (0..`kHeatHazeMaxVolumes` = 8) and `HeatHazeVolume volumes[kHeatHazeMaxVolumes]`, each `{ vec3 center; f32 radius; f32 strength; f32 rise; f32 scale; f32 softness; }` (world space; defaults radius 1, strength 0.5, rise 0.6, scale 1.0, softness 0.5; ranges `apply_quality` clamps: radius 0.05..50, strength 0..2, rise 0..4, scale 0.25..4, softness 0..1; NaN falls back to the default and a volume with a non-finite centre is dropped). `scene3d.h` and `forward3d.h` are over the 150 warning, so 43a is the seam PR for both, data only, merged first, with the `include_fanout.py` numbers: `Forward3DStats::heat_haze_volumes` and the private members 43c needs (`haze_pipe_`, `record_heat_haze`), the handle in the destroy list.
- **Pure CPU helpers (render, 43b).** Narrow header `mce/render/heat_haze.h` (math only, forward-declares `struct HeatHaze;`), bodies in `src/heat_haze.cpp`; f32, fixed order, no `rand`:
  - `bool heat_haze_project(const mat4& view_proj, const mat4& view, vec3 center, f32 radius, HazeScreen& out)`: the volume's screen-space centre (uv), its screen radius (as a fraction of the view height, from the projected sphere) and its nearest view depth (`dist - radius`, clamped to the near plane); false when the sphere is fully behind the camera, fully off screen or the input is not finite.
  - `u32 heat_haze_frame(const HeatHaze&, const mat4& view_proj, const mat4& view, f32 aspect, f32 time_s, HeatHazeFrame& out)`: culls volumes, sorts nothing (the shader sums the displacements), returns the number kept; `time_s` is the renderer's wrapped scene clock.
  - `f32 heat_haze_wetness`-style pure falloff: `heat_haze_falloff(f32 d_over_r, f32 softness)` = `1 - smoothstep(1 - softness, 1, d_over_r)` clamped (1 inside, 0 outside the sphere, a soft rim), pinned by tests.
  - `u32 heat_haze_hash(u32 x, u32 y, u32 seed)`: the integer hash the noise in the shader mirrors, pinned by goldens as in M-LENSRAIN 42b.
- **Quality tiers (render, 43b).** `QualitySettings::heat_haze` (Low false, Medium and High true) and `heat_haze_volumes` (Medium 4, High 8), cvar `r.heat_haze` (bool, Saved, "Heat haze (PostFx::haze; off = the frame without it)") next to `r.lens_rain` in `quality.cpp`; `apply_quality` clamps the ranges and trims the count to the tier cap.
- **Kit component (kit, 43d).** Opt-in `HeatHaze3D` (`mce/kit/heat_haze.h`, any number of entities, the first eight in ECS order (by entity id) that pass the quality cap are read; registered by `install_kit3d` next to `LensRain3D`; a separate component so `kit3d.h` stays untouched). Fields (all `Save`): `enabled` 1, `radius`, `strength`, `rise`, `scale`, `softness`, `offset_y` (0, -50..50: the centre sits this far above the entity's world position, so a haze volume can be placed on a fire entity and float above it), `fire_follow` 0 (0..1: when above 0 and the entity has a `Light3D`/point light of the kit, the strength is scaled by the light's intensity relative to its range so a flickering fire hazes more when bright; the multiplier is a pure function, tested), explicit zeroed `pad_`. No runtime state: it never hashes anything. `Kit3DExtract` (`.cpp` only) fills `post.haze` from the entities' world transforms and runs it through `render::apply_quality`.
- **Determinism.** The haze is a pure function of the camera, the volumes' world positions, the settings, this frame's depth and the scene clock; it never feeds the simulation. A scene without the component is byte-identical.

## Pass design (43c)

- **Where it sits.** `record_heat_haze(cmd, scene)` runs right after `record_lens_rain` and before `record_post`. Same ping-pong as the flare and the lens rain: reads `post_source_`, writes `post_fx_target(post_size_)`, then `post_source_` = that target (never in place: TAA history). Skipped for render-to-texture views (`out_.linear_output`), like the lens rain; `count == 0` returns before recording anything.
- **Depth.** The main depth buffer is not sampled, so the pass reads the half-resolution opaque prepass `ao_gbuf_` (`a` = view depth, 0 for sky), calling `record_prepass` (a no-op when SSAO, SSR, contact shadows, water, the flare or the outline already drew it this frame). Cost when none of those is on: one half-resolution opaque prepass; recorded in the risks.
- **Shader (`kHazeFS`, GLSL 450).** `HazeU` (bank `kPass`): `count`, `time`, `aspect`, the sun-free parameters plus `kHeatHazeMaxVolumes` × 2 `vec4` (centre uv + near depth + screen radius; strength, rise, scale, softness) = 4 + 16 `vec4`. For each of the (constant bound 8, early-`break` on `count`) volumes: `d = distance(uv_aspect, centre_uv) / screen_radius`, `w = falloff(d, softness)`; the depth mask `m = smoothstep(near - 0.5, near + 0.5, scene_depth)` read from the prepass (sky counts as far, so haze bends the horizon), `offset += noise_dir(uv * scale / radius, time * rise) * strength * w * m * 0.02` (view heights), where `noise_dir` is a two-octave value noise built on `heat_haze_hash`, rising with `time * rise`. The final lookup is `textureLod(src, uv + offset, 0)`; a lookup that lands on a pixel *nearer* than the volume (an occluder the displacement would pull in) falls back to the undisplaced pixel, which keeps the front object's silhouette sharp (one extra prepass tap, constant bound).
- **Per backend.** Plain GLSL 450 like every pass; the banks the post passes use (`kAlbedo` for the source, `kNormal` for the prepass), one uniform block, no SSBO, no compute, no derivatives in non-uniform flow, explicit `textureLod`/`texelFetch`, constant loop bounds.

## Risks

- **Look.** Displacement amplitude, noise scale and rise speed are look choices only a real display can judge; llvmpipe pixels prove placement, masking, determinism and the off checks. Goes to the GPU list (row A16).
- **Depth mask edge.** The half-resolution prepass can mis-mask a thin occluder; the nearer-pixel fallback keeps silhouettes, a thin pole may show a one-pixel halo. Recorded.
- **Transparent occluders.** Transparent geometry writes no depth, so haze bends what is behind glass but also glass in front of a volume. Recorded as a limit.
- **Prepass cost.** With SSAO, SSR, contact shadows, water, the flare and the outline all off, haze alone triggers the half-resolution opaque prepass; the pass only runs while a volume is on screen.

## GPU verification

Headless (llvmpipe, lavapipe): off equals the plain frame exactly; a volume on screen changes pixels only inside its screen disc (plus the soft rim); the same time gives the same frame, a different time another; a pole in front of the volume stays sharp (its pixels are unchanged) while the checker behind it is displaced; a volume behind the camera or fully off screen records nothing; count above the tier cap is trimmed; the TAA history stays free of haze. ## Follow-ups

- 43c as built (2026-11-06), where it differs from the plan above: `HazeU` is 1 + 16 `vec4` (272 bytes): `h_frame` = count, aspect and the projection's ray slopes `1 / proj[0][0]`, `1 / proj[1][1]` (0 for an orthographic view), then per volume (centre uv, near distance, screen radius) and (strength, rise phase, scale, softness). The time does not ride the block: the CPU turns `time * rise * kHazeCells * scale` into a per-volume phase in lattice cells wrapped to the lattice's 256-row period (`kHazePeriodCells`; rows `& 255u` in the shader), so the rising field repeats without a pop and its coordinates stay small. The depth mask compares a distance along the pixel's ray (the prepass view depth times the ray length from the slopes) with `heat_haze_project`'s near depth, which is a distance, not a view z (without it the mask would be up to ~20 % short at the frame's corners). The noise is `kHazeCells` = 3 cells per screen radius at scale 1, two octaves (2x frequency, half weight), two channels, seeds `4 * volume + channel`. The nearer-pixel fallback blends over the same 1 m band as the mask (`smoothstep(nearest - 0.5, nearest + 0.5, lookup distance)`, `nearest` the nearest contributing volume). The pass uses the unjittered projection (it runs on the resolved frame). The pipeline is created only where the prepass pipeline exists (SSAO, SSR, contact shadows or the edge outline on: the outline is on by default); without it the haze records nothing, like the flare. The GLSL hash is the macro `MCE_GLSL_HEAT_HAZE_HASH`, so the GL test runs it alone on the GPU and compares 64 hashes with `heat_haze_hash` bit for bit (GL and SDL GPU). `test_render_gl` gained the include path `src/engine/render/src` for that. `scene3d.h` and `forward3d.h` were not touched.
- `include_fanout.py` after 43a/43b (measured 2026-11-06 on the seam branch, test files included; upper bounds): `scene3d.h` 201 of 1015 TUs (WARNING, the seam PR), `forward3d.h` 177 (WARNING, the seam PR), `quality.h` 132, the new `mce/render/heat_haze.h` 2 (`heat_haze.cpp` and `test_heat_haze.cpp`; the later includers `forward3d.cpp`, `quality.cpp`, the extract and install add a few more). No later step touches `scene3d.h` or `forward3d.h`.
- `heat_haze_hash` is `h = mix(x); h = mix(h ^ y * 0x85ebca6b); h = mix(h ^ seed * 0xc2b2ae35)` with the lowbias32 `mix` of `lens_rain_hash`; the GLSL port in 43c must match the goldens in `tests/render/test_heat_haze.cpp`.
- `heat_haze_project` clamps the nearest depth to `kHeatHazeNearPlane` (0.05 m) and drops a volume whose centre is behind the camera plane while the camera is outside it (a sliver at the screen edge is lost, conservative); a camera inside a sphere gives screen radius 2 (covers the screen).
