## GPU tests and goldens
- The thread ran test_render_gl, test_kit_gl and test_ui_layout by hand under `xvfb-run`, on GL and `MCE_RHI=sdlgpu`. Each filtered run took seconds, not minutes. I don't know the time for the full suites.

- llvmpipe and lavapipe gave the same numbers in the render-texture tests. GL and SDL GPU player captures differ by up to 32 levels, near the particles.

## Skipping GPU tests on non-render changes
- Probably safe for changes that touch neither src/engine/{render,rhi,kit,ui}, nor src/player, nor src/editor.
- Risky otherwise. Render behavior lives outside src/engine/render too: kit extracts and views, the player's frame loop, editor preview. A path filter limited to "render/" would have missed bugs in this thread, for example a render-texture orientation issue that was fixed in kit and player code.

## Disk and ccache
- The session's disk allowance is about 39 GB. A `--batch` run filled it: every stage after walls failed with "No space left on device", and the failure looked like build errors.
- Tree sizes: linux-gcc-debug 14 GB, linux-clang-debug 7.9 GB (tidy), linux-gcc-release 3.5 GB, linux-clang-release 1.9 GB, build/_fetch 0.9 GB, ccache 1.5 GB. linux-asan comes on top of these.
- Workaround used: run the batch stages one at a time and delete each tree after its stage.
- Suggestion: have `--batch` check free space first, or delete the clang and asan trees after their stages.
- ccache hit rates stay low (12-42 %) after merges that touch widely included headers. Each step here touched kit/render headers.
