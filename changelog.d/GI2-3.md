- M-GI2 50e: the irradiance volume's visibility lookup. A volume with `GiVolume::visibility` now weighs each of a
  point's 8 probes by what the probe can see: per axis, the point's distance along it against the probe's record on
  that side (Chebyshev's bound, cubed, crushed below 0.2), the smallest of the three; a probe that sees mostly back faces
  (a share of 25 % or more: inside a solid or a closed shell) weighs nothing, and a point no probe sees takes the sky's
  ambient. A thin wall no longer leaks a sunlit room's light into a dark one (new golden `gi_thin_wall`), a probe
  buried in a solid or under a street slab no longer darkens what is near it. The record's distances are now taken
  along each lobe's axis (a plane across the axis reads its own distance). Limits: sound for walls across an axis
  (oblique thin walls still leak), openings such as doorways are read as their whole wall, no occlusion in the fade
  band outside the box. New cvar `r.gi.visibility` (default 1; 0 = the plain lookup, the layout and a bake kept, the
  volume refills once) and `Forward3DStats::gi_visibility`. Volumes without the field render byte-identically.
