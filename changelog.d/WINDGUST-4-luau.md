- Luau wind gusts and the weather sample's `WindGust`, M-WINDGUST step 12d (milestone closed): `weather.gust_strength()`, `gust_min_gap()`, `gust_max_gap()`, `gust_min_rise()`, `gust_max_rise()`, `gust_min_fall()`, `gust_max_fall()`, `gust_min_peak()`, `gust_max_peak()`, `gust_seed()`, `gust_affects()` and `gust_enabled()` read the first `WindGust` (`mce/kit/wind_gust.h`) and `weather.set_gust{strength=, min_gap=, max_gap=, min_rise=, max_rise=, min_fall=, max_fall=, min_peak=, max_peak=, seed=, affects=, enabled=}` writes it; `weather.gust_level()` (`gust_q` over 2^24) and `weather.effective_wind()` (the gusted wind with every consumer bit applied) are read-only.
