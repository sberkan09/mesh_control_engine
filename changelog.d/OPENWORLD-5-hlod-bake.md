- Kit, editor, MCP: the HLOD bake (M-OPENWORLD A3). `kit::bake_chunk_hlod` merges an area scene's visible rigid meshes (opaque or masked materials, one submesh per material, each source's LOD levels kept) into one proxy relative to its `SceneChunk`; `EditorSession::bake_hlod` and the `hlod_bake` tool write it to `assets/hlod/<scene>/chunk_<id>.mchlod` and point the chunk's `ChunkLod3D.proxy` at it as one undo step. Meshes under `min_size` (default 1 m) are left out; the same scene bakes byte-identical.
