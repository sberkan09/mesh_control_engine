- M-GI2 50f + 50g: probe relocation and the camera-following irradiance volume. With `GiVolume::relocate` a probe
  found inside a large rigid draw's box is captured just outside it (0.1 m past the nearest face, at most `max_offset`
  of a cell, never into another solid; one deeper stays and is classified off), the offset stored in the probe's record
  and read by the lookup; computed on the CPU when the volume fills and for each scrolled-in slab
  (`Forward3DStats::gi_relocated`). Off by default: a hollow mesh (a room) counts as solid at its box. A relocating
  volume with rolling updates fills at runtime (its bake is loaded only without updates, the Low tier). With
  `GiVolume::follow` the volume's box follows the frame's first camera on x and z in whole cells, in world coordinates
  (a floating-origin rebase moves nothing), moving once the camera strays 0.75 cell from its centre (a camera swaying
  across a cell edge does not scroll back and forth): each probe keeps its texture slot (toroidal addressing), a step
  invalidates only the slab that scrolled in (those probes weigh nothing until captured, nearest the camera first,
  sharing the frame's capture budget with the rolling update; `Forward3DStats::gi_scrolled`), a jump of the whole box
  refills. A following volume is filled at runtime (its bake is used only where it does not follow, the Low tier; the
  editor's bake clears `follow`); a later view of the frame neither moves it nor draws with it. `r.gi.visibility 0`
  switches relocation off with the visibility; following stays. Volumes without the fields render byte-identically.
