- Autumn leaf feed, M-LEAFFALL step 21b: `Kit3DExtract` reads the first `LeafFall` (with the first `Season`, `WorldClock`, `Weather`, `WindGust` and `WindDirection`; day 0 hour 0 without a clock, the manual floor without a `Season`, calm without a `Weather`) and, at an amount above 0, adds one local `render::ParticleEmitter` from `leaf_fall_look` (id `0xFFFF0001`, above the camera by `height_m`, following it, alpha blended, no collision), counted in `stats_.particle_emitters`. `LeafFall`, `Season`, `WorldClock`, `Weather` and every `ParticleEmitter3D` are never written; a scene with no `LeafFall`, `enabled` 0, `strength` 0 or an amount of 0 adds nothing and renders as before. `QualitySettings` has no particle field, so the Low tier halves the rate and the particle budget. No shader or render-struct change.
