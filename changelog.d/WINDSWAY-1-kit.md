- Foliage sways with the weather's wind in the kit, M-WINDSWAY step 1: new `WindSway` component (`mce/kit/wind_sway.h`, one per scene: `amplitude_gain` 4, `frequency_gain` 1.5, `gust_gain` 0.6, `flutter_gain` 3), registered by `install_weather`. Only a scene with both a `Weather` and a `WindSway` changes: `Kit3DExtract` scales the `Foliage3D` and `TerrainFoliage3D` batches' amplitude, frequency, gust and flutter by `Weather.wind` (`1 + (gain - 1) * wind`); the foliage components are never written and the scatter cache is not invalidated. Every other scene extracts exactly as before. User guide 9.19.
