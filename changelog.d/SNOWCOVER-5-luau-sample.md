- Luau snow cover and the weather sample snows, M-SNOWCOVER step 6d (milestone closed): `weather.snow_cover()` reads the engine-written cover (0..1) of the first `SnowCover` (`mce/kit/snow_cover.h`); `weather.snow_strength()`, `snow_build()`, `snow_melt()`, `snow_coverage()`, `snow_scale()` and `snow_wind()` read the authoring fields and `weather.set_snow{strength=, build=, melt=, coverage=, scale=, wind=}` writes any of them (every key optional, values clamped to the component's Range).
