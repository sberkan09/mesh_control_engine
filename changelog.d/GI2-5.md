- M-GI2 50h: the irradiance volume's new features are authorable. A new opt-in kit component `GiVisibility3D`
  (`mce/kit/gi_visibility.h`: `enabled`, `relocate`, `max_offset` 0..0.45, `follow_camera`, `bake_format`) on the
  `GiVolume3D`'s entity turns on probe visibility (the 10-texel record), relocation and the camera-following box;
  `Kit3DExtract` copies it onto `render::GiVolume` and widens the off-screen capture reach to the following box. The
  editor's GI bake stamps the record format into the component and a volume ignores a bake of another format. A scene
  without the component hashes, saves and draws exactly as before (`GiVolume3D` is unchanged).
