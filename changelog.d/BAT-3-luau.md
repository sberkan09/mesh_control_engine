- Luau dusk bats, M-BAT step 30c: `weather.set_dusk_bat{enabled=, window_full=, window_end=, manual=, dusk_low=, dusk_high=, wind_calm=, wind_still=, rate=, box_xz_m=, height_m=, size_m=, lifetime_s=, speed=, color={r,g,b}, max_particles=, seed=, strength=}` writes the first `DuskBat` (every key optional, clamped to the component's Range; an unknown key, a non-number, a colour that is not a table of three numbers or a scene without a `DuskBat` raises a named error and writes nothing; it never adds the component), the getters `weather.dusk_bat_<key>()` (`weather.dusk_bat_color()` returns `r, g, b`) and the read-only `weather.dusk_bat_flying()`, the dusk bat amount 0..1 from the kit helper with the season phase of the first `Season` at the first `WorldClock`, the night fade of the `Environment3D` hour, the effective wind (with the gust sway) and the rain of the first `Weather` (the same amount the renderer draws). Typed in `mce.d.luau`. `samples/12_weather` logs `dusk_bat:` every 150 ticks beside `butterfly:`; its expectations move, nothing else.
