- Timed lights, M-DAYNIGHT step 3: `TimedLight3D` (`mce/kit/timed_light.h`) on an entity with a `PointLight3D`, `SpotLight3D` and/or `MeshRef` fades the light and the emissive tint on at `on_hour` and off at `off_hour` (wraps midnight, `fade_minutes`), following the first `WorldClock`; the authored intensity is captured in `light_scale`/`spot_scale` and never drifts, and an off light has `enabled` 0. Events `DuskStarted` and `DawnStarted`. System `kit_timed_light` runs after `kit_day_cycle`. Opt-in; user guide 9.18.
