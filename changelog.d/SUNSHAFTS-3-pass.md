- Sun shafts pass and `SunShafts3D`, M-SUNSHAFTS steps 45c and 45d: with `PostFx::shafts.enabled` and the sun in front of the camera above the horizon, Forward3D adds god rays right after the exposure histogram and before the lens flare (after TAA, depth of field and motion blur; before the underwater look, the lens rain, the haze, bloom and the tonemapper): a half-resolution pass walks from every pixel toward the sun over the HDR frame and the half-resolution opaque prepass (16 samples on Medium, 32 on High, a fixed per-pixel jitter), counting only sky samples brighter than `threshold` (the sun disk; opaque geometry occludes, clouds dim the disk), and a full-resolution composite adds the soft-clamped rays (never above `cap` per channel), veiled by distance on geometry. The pass backs off to 0.4 where the volumetric fog is drawn and fades out under water; fills `Forward3DStats::sun_shafts` / `sun_shafts_strength`. The setting off, the Low tier, `r.sun_shafts 0`, a sun behind the camera or below the horizon and render-to-texture views record nothing, so the frame is unchanged bit for bit. The optional `SunShafts3D` component (`mce/kit/sun_shafts.h`: `enabled`, `intensity`, `length`, `decay`, `threshold`, `cap`, `cloud_follow`, `scatter_m`; the first enabled one by entity index is read) switches it on through the Kit3DExtract feed (cloud cover dims it by `cloud_follow`); the sun and the fog are the scene's own. The default `threshold` 1 is measured: the sky stays at or under 0.65 HDR luminance and the disk at 6 (atmosphere, sun 4 degrees up) to 23.6 (gradient sky). Limits: the pass needs the prepass pipeline (SSAO, SSR, contact shadows or the edge outline on at startup; one log warning otherwise); screen space only (an off-screen or transparent caster casts no shaft, and the rays leave with the disk at the border); an orthographic view is unsupported.
