- Thin-surface translucency and wrap lighting drawn, M-TRANSLUCENT steps 41d and 41e (TRANSLUCENT-4): a material with `translucency` lets a share of its diffuse through from the back, tinted by its own colour (a leaf, a petal, a paper screen or a lamp shade lit from behind glows), as `KHR_materials_diffuse_transmission` mixes it (a front-lit translucent leaf is no brighter than before), for the sun (with its own back shadow, so a leaf behind another leaf stays dark, and the sun's cookie), for point and spot lights (their shadow and cookie included) and, on Medium and High, for the ambient seen through the back (the sky's SH9 irradiance, the hemisphere gradient or the irradiance volume along the inverted normal). On Medium and High a forward-scatter lobe brightens the glow where the view looks into the light. A material with `wrap` wraps the diffuse of the sun and the local lights past the terminator (a soft edge for skin, wax, cloth, fruit). Metal does not transmit, snow on its upper face damps it seen from either side, unlit and terrain materials ignore it; instanced foliage batches draw it as the single draws. `Forward3DStats::translucent_draws` counts the draws that carry it; `r.translucency 0` or `Forward3DDesc::translucency = false` draws the plain frame bit for bit. No new texture, buffer, pass or sampler; an off draw pays one per-draw compare.
