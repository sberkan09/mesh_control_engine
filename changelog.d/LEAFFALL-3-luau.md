- Luau leaf fall, M-LEAFFALL step 21c: `weather.set_leaf_fall{enabled=, autumn_start=, autumn_full=, autumn_end_full=, autumn_end=, manual=, rate=, box_xz_m=, height_m=, size_m=, lifetime_s=, fall_speed=, wind_follow=, leaf_color={r,g,b}, tint_follow=, max_particles=, seed=, strength=}` writes the first `LeafFall` (every key optional, clamped to the component's Range; an unknown key, a non-number, a colour that is not a table of three numbers or a scene without a `LeafFall` raises a named error and writes nothing; it never adds the component), the getters `weather.leaf_fall_<key>()` (`weather.leaf_fall_leaf_color()` returns `r, g, b`) and the read-only `weather.leaf_falling()`, the leaf amount 0..1 from the kit helper on the first `Season` at the first `WorldClock`'s day and hour (the same amount the renderer draws; the manual floor with no `Season`, the fixed start phase with no clock). Typed in `mce.d.luau`. `samples/12_weather` sets `manual` 0.6 on its `LeafFall` (its Season never reaches autumn in the run) and logs `leaves:` every 150 ticks; its expectations move, nothing else.
