- Flock feed, M-BIRDFLOCK step 26b: `Kit3DExtract` reads the first `Flock`, the first `Season` and `WorldClock` (phase), the sun height from the environment (`time_of_day`, else the fixed `sun_direction`) and the rain of the first `Weather` (Rain and Storm only) and, at an amount above 0, adds one local alpha-blended `render::ParticleEmitter` from `flock_look` (id `0xFFFF0005`, in a box `height_m` above the camera following it, no collision), counted in `stats_.particle_emitters`. `Flock`, `Season`, `WorldClock`, `Weather` and every `ParticleEmitter3D` are never written; a scene with no `Flock`, `enabled` 0, `strength` 0 or an amount of 0 (midday, night, rain) adds nothing and renders as before. The Low tier halves the rate and the particle budget. No shader or render-struct change.
