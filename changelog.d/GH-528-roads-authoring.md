- Road networks (M-OPENWORLD B1 authoring): MCP `roads_generate` writes a street grid as a `.mcroads`, and
  `roads_info` reports what a network cooks to and what traffic would trip on: one-way lanes that end
  nowhere, and lanes cut off from the rest of the network. The editor draws road lanes while a 3D game plays
  (View > Roads). Traffic stops for pedestrians crossing in front (M-OPENWORLD B2 with B3 crowds).
