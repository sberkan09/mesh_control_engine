- Butterfly feed, M-BUTTERFLY step 27b: `Kit3DExtract` reads the first `Butterfly`, the first `Season` and `WorldClock` (phase), the night fade of the day clock, the effective wind and the rain of the first `Weather` (Rain and Storm only) and, at an amount above 0, adds one local alpha-blended `render::ParticleEmitter` from `butterfly_look` (id `0xFFFF0006`, a low box `box_xz_m` by `height_m` centred on the camera following it, no collision), counted in `stats_.particle_emitters`. `Butterfly`, `Season`, `WorldClock`, `Weather` and every `ParticleEmitter3D` are never written; a scene with no `Butterfly`, `enabled` 0, `strength` 0 or an amount of 0 (winter, night, strong wind, rain) adds nothing and renders as before. The Low tier halves the rate and the particle budget. No shader or render-struct change.
