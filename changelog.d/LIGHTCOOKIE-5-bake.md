- Light cookie atlas, M-LIGHTCOOKIE step 40c: `Forward3D` bakes each distinct spot-light cookie texture of a frame into one layer of a shared atlas (the tier's 128 / 256 / 512 texels, its mip chain packed beside it in the layer, since the RHI renders into a layer and a viewport but not into a mip; one fullscreen draw per level, only when a layer's texture, generation or residency changed), uploads the cookie records to `rhi::bank::kLightCookies` and fills `Forward3DStats::cookie_lights` / `cookie_layers`. The atlas shares sampler bank 2 with the third decal texture: a frame with a cookie draws decals from at most two textures (a third texture's decals are dropped with a one-time warning); a frame without one binds bank 2 as before. `-r.light_cookies 0` at startup creates no bake pipeline or atlas; the cvar and the tier are also read every frame. Frames without cookies are bit-identical.
