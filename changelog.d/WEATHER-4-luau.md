- Luau `weather` global, M-WEATHER step 4a: thin over the first `Weather` component (`mce/kit/weather.h`), no state of its own. `weather.kind()` (kind in effect) and `weather.target()` as strings, `weather.set(kind, transition_seconds?)` writes `Weather.target` (and `transition_seconds`), `weather.intensity()`, `weather.wind()`, `weather.wetness()`. A world without a Weather, an unknown kind or a NaN raise a named Luau error. Typed in `mce.d.luau`; user guide 27.3.
