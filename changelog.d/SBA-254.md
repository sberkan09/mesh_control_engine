- Window interior mapping (M-VISUAL4 step 5, SBA-254): a material with `"interior_cells": 1 | 2 | 4` (`.mcmat`) or the glTF material extra `mce_interior_cells` shows a room behind each glossy (glass) texel of that window grid: the view ray is slab-tested against a per-cell room box (hashed wall tint and depth, floor, ceiling, back wall) in the forward mesh shader, and the window's own emission lights the room. Off by default (0): every other material draws byte for byte as before. The open-world sample's upper-floor facades use it. `docs/milestones/M-VISUAL4.md`.
