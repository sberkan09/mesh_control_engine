- Underwater pass, M-UNDERWATER step 44c: with a water plane on (`Scene3D::water()`) and the camera below its mean level (plus a crossing band wider than the swell), Forward3D turns the frame into an underwater picture right after the flare and before the lens rain, the heat haze and bloom (after TAA, depth of field, motion blur and the exposure histogram, so the look neither enters the TAA history nor pumps the exposure): per-channel Beer-Lambert absorption over the distance from the half-resolution opaque prepass (red first), the water's scatter colour as fog, a gentle whole-cycle screen wobble (one octave at Medium, two at High), and, for up-going rays that meet the surface first, a lid with a bright Snell window straight up and a dark fog-coloured mirror beyond the critical angle. The crossing blends with the submersion, so it has no pop. Low (or a renderer without the prepass pipeline, said once) draws a flat tint with no prepass. Fills `Forward3DStats::underwater` / `underwater_submersion`. No water, the camera above the band, `PostFx::underwater.enabled` false, strength 0, `r.underwater 0` or a render-to-texture view record no pass and create nothing, so the frame is unchanged bit for bit; the pipeline is compiled on the first frame that needs it. Kit-side check (44d, no component): the existing `Water` feed reaches the pass's inputs unchanged and nothing is written to the world.
