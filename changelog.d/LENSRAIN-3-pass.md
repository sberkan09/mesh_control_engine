- Lens rain pass, M-LENSRAIN step 42c: with `PostFx::rain.enabled` and a wet `amount`, Forward3D draws water drops on the camera lens right after the lens flare and before bloom (after TAA, depth of field, motion blur and the exposure histogram, so the drops neither pump the exposure nor enter the TAA history): procedural drops on one (Medium) or two (High) screen-fixed grids picked by `lens_rain_hash`, each a lens that shows the scene around it mirrored (the sampled radius growing to a bright rim), trails behind the big drops as they fall at about `fall_speed` view heights a minute, a tent blur of the wet glass between them, drops that fade in and out with the wetness and over their fall cycle on the scene clock (repeating every 600 s without a pop), and fills `Forward3DStats::lens_rain` / `lens_rain_amount`. Off (the default), amount 0, the Low tier, `r.lens_rain 0` or a render-to-texture view record no pass, so the frame is unchanged bit for bit.
