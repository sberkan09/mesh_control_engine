- Wet surfaces in the kit, M-WETSURFACE step 5c: new `WetSurfaces` component (`mce/kit/wet_surfaces.h`, one per scene: `strength`, `darken`, `wet_roughness`, `puddles`, `puddle_scale_m`), registered by `install_weather`. `Kit3DExtract` hands the scene `Weather.wetness * strength` (through `apply_quality`) only when the scene has both a `Weather` and a `WetSurfaces`; every other scene stays dry and its state hash is unchanged (`Weather` is untouched).
