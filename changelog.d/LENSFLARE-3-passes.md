- Sun lens flare passes, M-LENSFLARE step 38c: with `PostFx::flare.enabled` and the sun on screen, Forward3D adds a soft halo, tinted ghost discs along the sun-to-centre axis and (High only) a starburst after depth of field, motion blur and the exposure histogram and before bloom, occluded by a 32-tap 1x1 sample of the half-resolution opaque prepass depth around the sun (opaque geometry only; the prepass is drawn for it when SSAO, SSR, contact shadows and the water did not, and the flare is skipped when `Forward3DDesc` turns all three screen-space effects off). It sits on the sky's sun disk (the light's direction) and fills `Forward3DStats::lens_flare` / `lens_flare_strength` (the CPU strength, before the GPU occlusion). Off (the default), the Low tier, `r.lens_flare 0`, a sun behind the camera, below the horizon or off screen record no pass, so the frame is unchanged bit for bit.
