- Kit: city traffic on road networks (M-OPENWORLD B2). `TrafficDriver3D` drives a `Vehicle3D` along a placed `.mcroads`
  network: it keeps its lane, turns at junctions, follows the car ahead, stops at red lights and stop signs and gives way
  at junctions. `TrafficSpawner3D` keeps cars alive around the player (a prefab or the game's `TrafficSpawnHook`).
