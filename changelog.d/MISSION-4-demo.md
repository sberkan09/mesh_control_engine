- Missions demo, M-MISSION step 4 (closes M-MISSION): `samples/10_missions` is a no-code flat-field game with three chained missions (reach a zone, kill a `Health3D` target with the player's `Weapon3D`, deliver within a time limit), objective markers on the `Minimap` of a `.mcui` HUD fed by the stock player, and a Luau script that starts mission 1 and chains the next ones with `mission.*` and `marker.*`. `ctest -R sample_missions` pins the completion ticks, a time-limit failure, a save/load in the middle of a mission and the replay hash. `Mission`, `Objective`, `MapMarker`, `Health3D` and `Weapon3D` leave `sample_coverage.allow`; user guide 9.14 and 18.
