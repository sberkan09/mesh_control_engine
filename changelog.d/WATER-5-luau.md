- Luau water, M-WATER step 15d (Luau half): the `water` table over the first `Water` (`mce/kit/water.h`). `water.set{level=, half_extent=, wave_count=, amplitude=, wavelength=, steepness=, wind_dir=, wind_follow=, speed_scale=, shallow_color={r,g,b}, deep_color={r,g,b}, depth_scale=, reflectivity=, roughness=, foam_amount=, foam_width=, enabled=}` writes it (every key optional, clamped to the component's Range; an unknown key, a non-number, a colour that is not three numbers or a scene without a `Water` raises a named error and writes nothing; the setter never adds the component), the getters carry the key names (`water.level()`, `water.shallow_color()` returns `r, g, b`, ...), and the read-only `water.height_at(x, z)`, `water.normal_at(x, z)` (`x, y, z`) and `water.submerged(x, y, z)` evaluate the drawn waves at the scene time `tick * dt` with the renderer's main direction, so a float follows exactly what is on screen. New kit helpers `water_height_at` / `water_normal_at` (a `Water`, a position and a raw scene time). Typed in `mce.d.luau`, guide section 9.19m. The sample is the next step.
