- Luau meteors, M-METEOR step 18d: `weather.set_meteor{rate=, brightness=, length=, speed=, min_darkness=, night_only=, seed=, enabled=}` writes the first `Meteor` (every key optional, clamped to the component's Range; an unknown key, a non-number or a scene without a `Meteor` raises a named error and writes nothing; it never adds the component), the getters (`weather.meteor_rate()`, `weather.meteor_min_darkness()`, ...) and the read-only `weather.meteor_level()` from the kit helper with the extract's night fade, cloud cover and rain (0 with no clock unless `night_only` is 0). Typed in `mce.d.luau`, guide 9.19p and 27.3. `samples/12_weather` (whose Weather entity already carries a `Meteor`) logs `meteor:` every 150 ticks; only its expectations move.
