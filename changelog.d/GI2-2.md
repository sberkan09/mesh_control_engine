- M-GI2 50c + 50d: the irradiance volume's per-probe record. New narrow header `mce/render/gi_probes.h` (pure helpers
  `gi_record_at`, `gi_chebyshev`, `gi_lobe_weight`, `gi_dmax`, `gi_relocate`, `gi_follow_origin`, the slot packing, the
  record's constants and `GiProbeState`). A volume with `GiVolume::visibility` stores 10 texels a probe: its fill runs a
  geometry-only vis pass per face (`kGiVisFS`, signed distances, back faces drawn) and `kGiProjectFS` writes the six-lobe
  distance record and the back-face share into texels 4..9; rolling updates leave them bit for bit. Nothing reads the
  record yet (50e), so frames do not change; volumes without the new fields render byte-identically. `kMeshFS` is 3.6 KB
  shorter (GLSL comments moved into C++ comments), leaving room for the visibility lookup.
