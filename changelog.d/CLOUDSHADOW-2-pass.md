- Cloud shadows pass, M-CLOUDSHADOW step 51c: with `PostFx::cloud_shadow.enabled` (after `apply_quality`), a sky cloud layer with coverage and the sun at least about 1 degree up, Forward3D darkens the ground, buildings and other opaque geometry under the sky's clouds right after the exposure histogram and before the sun shafts (after TAA, depth of field and motion blur, so the shade never enters the TAA history or pumps the exposure). One fullscreen pass (`kCloudShadowFS`) rebuilds each pixel's camera-relative position from the half-resolution opaque prepass, follows the sun to the cloud plane and reads the sky's own noise there (the same `p_cloud` / `p_cloud2` values and the same integer-hash noise as the sky, shared through `MCE_GLSL_CLOUD_NOISE`; 2 octaves on Medium, 3 on High), then multiplies the pixel by `cloud_shadow_factor` (1 - strength * density * sun share * the elevation fade); under the exp² fog only the surface part of the pixel is shaded (`cloud_shadow_shade`, exact for the main pass's src = s T + fog (1 - T)), and the new `cloud_shadow_fog_visibility` and `cloud_shadow_perspective` helpers give the fog share and the view gate. The new `cloud_shadow_sun_share(sun_color, intensity, ambient_sky)` in `mce/render/cloud_shadow.h` gives the sun's share of the light (S / (S + A) of the sun's and the sky ambient's luma), so a dim or dusk sun casts shallower shade. Sky pixels are untouched, the patches drift with the clouds' wind and clock, a floating-origin rebase changes nothing, and the shade fades out over the underwater crossing band; fills `Forward3DStats::cloud_shadow` / `cloud_shadow_strength`. The setting off, the sky pass off (`Sky::enabled` false), the Low tier, `r.cloud_shadows 0`, no coverage or the layer off, a sun under the fade band or below the horizon, an orthographic view and render-to-texture views record nothing, so the frame is unchanged bit for bit (every existing golden is unchanged). Limits: the pass needs the prepass pipeline (SSAO, SSR, contact shadows or the edge outline on at startup; one log warning otherwise); transparent surfaces and water get the shade of what the prepass saw behind them (or none); a silhouette pixel takes its half-resolution block's sample (the prepass depth is point sampled, and the pass runs after depth of field and motion blur, so blurred pixels take the sharp prepass's shade); the sun's share reads the light's sky ambient only, not the IBL or probe ambient, so under a bright IBL sky the shade is somewhat deeper than the light split warrants.
