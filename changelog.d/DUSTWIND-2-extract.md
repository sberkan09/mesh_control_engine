- Dust feed, M-DUSTWIND step 25b: `Kit3DExtract` reads the first `Dust`, the rain of the first `Weather` (Rain and Storm only) and the gusted scene wind (0..1) and wind direction as the leaf block and, at an amount above 0, adds one local alpha-blended `render::ParticleEmitter` from `dust_look` (id `0xFFFF0004`, in a box around the camera following it, no collision), counted in `stats_.particle_emitters`. `Dust`, `Weather` and every `ParticleEmitter3D` are never written; a scene with no `Dust`, `enabled` 0, `strength` 0 or an amount of 0 (calm, rain) adds nothing and renders as before. The Low tier halves the rate and the particle budget. No shader or render-struct change.
