- Day cycle, M-DAYNIGHT step 2: `DayCycle` (`mce/kit/day_cycle.h`), one per scene, keyed curves over the hour (8 key slots, default night/dawn/noon/dusk preset, linear interpolation with wrap-around at midnight) for sun colour and intensity, ambient sky and ground colour, fog colour and density, and exposure. One system (after `kit_world_clock`) reads `Environment3D.time_of_day` and writes those `Environment3D` fields; nothing is written without a clock or with `enabled` 0. Opt-in; user guide 9.17.
