- Luau snow sparkle, M-SNOWSPARKLE step 31c: `weather.set_snow_sparkle{enabled=, cover_low=, cover_full=, sun_low=, sun_high=, manual=, rate=, box_xz_m=, height_m=, size_m=, lifetime_s=, color={r,g,b}, max_particles=, seed=, strength=}` writes the first `SnowSparkle` (every key optional, clamped to the component's Range; an unknown key, a non-number, a colour that is not a table of three numbers or a scene without a `SnowSparkle` raises a named error and writes nothing; it never adds the component), the getters `weather.snow_sparkle_<key>()` (`weather.snow_sparkle_color()` returns `r, g, b`) and the read-only `weather.snow_sparkle_active()`, the snow sparkle amount 0..1 from the kit helper with the cover of the first `SnowCover`, the sun elevation of the `Environment3D` hour, the cloud kind factor times the intensity and the rain of the first `Weather` (the same amount the renderer draws). Typed in `mce.d.luau`. `samples/12_weather` logs `snow_sparkle:` every 150 ticks beside `dusk_bat:`; its expectations move, nothing else.
