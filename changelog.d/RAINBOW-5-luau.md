- Luau rainbow, M-RAINBOW step 17d: `weather.set_rainbow{strength=, band_width=, secondary=, min_wetness=, max_sun_elevation=, enabled=}` writes the first `Rainbow` (every key optional, clamped to the component's Range; an unknown key, a non-number or a scene without a `Rainbow` raises a named error and writes nothing; it never adds the component), the getters (`weather.rainbow_strength()`, `weather.rainbow_max_sun_elevation()`, ...) and the read-only `weather.rainbow_level()` from the kit helper with the extract's sun elevation, wetness, rain and cloud cover (0 without a `Weather`). Typed in `mce.d.luau`, guide 9.19o and 27.3. `samples/12_weather` (whose Weather entity already carries a `Rainbow`) logs `rainbow:` every 150 ticks; only its expectations move.
