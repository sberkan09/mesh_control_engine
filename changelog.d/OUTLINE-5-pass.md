- Edge outline pass, M-OUTLINE step 39c: with `PostFx::outline.enabled`, Forward3D draws ink lines on silhouettes (a view-depth jump, and geometry against the sky with `sky_silhouette`) and creases (a view-normal jump) of the opaque scene in one fullscreen pass over the half-resolution opaque prepass, after the MSAA resolve and before TAA (so TAA, depth of field, motion blur, bloom, the tonemapper and the grade act on the lines), fading with `fade_start` / `fade_end` and the exp² fog (`fog_follow`, not under `Volumetrics`), with 8 taps on Medium and High and the 4-tap cross on Low; `Forward3DStats::edge_outline` / `edge_outline_taps` report it. The prepass pipelines are now created for `Forward3DDesc::edge_outline` too (default on), so the outline and the lens flare also work on a renderer without SSAO, SSR and contact shadows; the water's depth read is unchanged. Off (the default), opacity 0, both weights 0, `r.edge_outline 0` or `Forward3DDesc::edge_outline` false record no pass, so the frame is unchanged bit for bit.
