- BC texture cook, M-CONTENT step 1b-ii (GitHub #385): `mce_cook --textures bc` (and `MCE_COOK_TEXTURES=bc` for desktop builds) cooks textures to BC7, BC5, BC4 or BC1 through bc7enc_rdo (pinned by commit, built with `-ffp-contract=off`, confined by new cook-only walls). The texture sidecar gains `"compress"`: `none`, `auto`, `bc7`, `bc5`, `bc4`, `bc1` or `astc`, and the cook mode decides what each produces (a missing key keeps every existing pack byte-identical; `auto` picks BC7 for colour, BC5 for normal maps, BC4 for grey data, with provisional thresholds). A texture whose sides are not multiples of 4 cooks to RGBA8 with a warning; `srgb` with BC5 or BC4 and `compress` on `.hdr`/`.exr` are cook errors. BC cook golden hashes are guaranteed on x86_64 only; `test_cook_bc_arch` (slow) checks other architectures. Runtime upload of BC textures is the next step.
