- Scene/ECS: floating origin (M-OPENWORLD A2). `FloatingOrigin3D` on a base-scene entity makes the stream pump rebase the world in whole `snap` cells when the focus gets farther than `threshold` from the origin; `ecs::rebase_world` moves root transforms, world transforms and `WorldPoint` fields without change stamps, and streamed chunks load in place after a rebase. Physics bodies, characters, world-anchored joints, the navmesh and crowd, static instances, camera rails and the 3D extract follow; the frame after a rebase renders as a camera cut and GPU particles restart. Scenes without the component are unchanged.
