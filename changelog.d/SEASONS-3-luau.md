- Luau seasons, M-SEASONS step 19c: `weather.set_season{enabled=, year_days=, start_phase=, spring_tint={r,g,b}, autumn_tint={r,g,b}, winter_tint={r,g,b}, winter_density=, snow_cover_bias=, cold_bias=, strength=}` writes the first `Season` (every key optional, clamped to the component's Range, tints 0..4 per channel; an unknown key, a non-number, a tint that is not a table of three numbers or a scene without a `Season` raises a named error and writes nothing; it never adds the component), the getters `weather.season_<key>()` (the three tints return `r, g, b`) and the read-only `weather.season_phase()` from the kit helper on the first `WorldClock`'s day and hour (the fixed `start_phase` with no clock). Typed in `mce.d.luau`. `samples/12_weather` sets `year_days` 1 on its `Season` (its 600 s day would make a 12-day year too slow to show) and logs `season:` every 150 ticks; only its expectations move.
